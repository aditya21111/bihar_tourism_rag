OVERVIEW:
Ashok Dham Mandir is a prominent temple complex located at Ashok Dham Rajauna Chouki in the Lakhisarai district of Bihar. At its center stands the Indradamneshwar Mahadev Mandir, dedicated to the primary deity Lord Shiva. This central temple is surrounded by three additional temples dedicated to Goddess Parvati, Nandi (the mount of Shiva), and Goddess Durga. The site has historical roots dating back to the 12th century when a temple was originally built here by King Indradyumna.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Ashok Dham temple complex is a major religious destination in Lakhisarai. The focal point of the complex is the Indradamneshwar Mahadev Mandir, which houses the primary deity, Lord Shiva. Encircling the main Shiva temple are three other significant temples dedicated to Goddess Parvati, Nandi, and Goddess Durga. The architecture and spiritual ambiance draw numerous devotees. The historical foundation of the site dates to the 12th century, linking the contemporary religious practices to ancient royal patronage by King Indradyumna.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The site is highly significant historically and religiously, originally featuring a temple built in the 12th century by King Indradyumna.
