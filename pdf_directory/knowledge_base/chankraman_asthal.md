OVERVIEW:
Chankraman Asthal in Bodhgaya marks a sacred path where Lord Buddha walked in meditation after attaining enlightenment. It is a highly revered site featuring stone carvings that commemorate the footprints of the awakened Buddha.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This sacred pathway is significant for its depiction of Buddha's meditative walking. At this asthal, Buddha's foot-prints are beautifully figured in black stone. Alongside the footprints, the site is adorned with 19 exquisite lotus carvings, representing the purity and spiritual bloom of his enlightenment.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It represents the period shortly after Buddha attained enlightenment when he walked around the sacred area. The carvings act as a prominent focus for pilgrim veneration and meditation.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to belief, after attaining enlightenment, Buddha spent time walking around the sacred place. Legend has it that lotus flowers bloomed wherever he placed his feet, which is commemorated by the 19 lotus carvings found at the site.
