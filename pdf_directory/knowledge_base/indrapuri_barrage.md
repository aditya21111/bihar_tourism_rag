OVERVIEW:
Indrapuri Barrage, also known as Sone Barrage, is situated across the Sone River in Rohtas. At 1,407 metres long, it is the fourth longest barrage in the world, renowned for its scenic beauty and tranquility.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Indrapuri Barrage is built across the Sone River in the Rohtas district. The Sone River originates near Amarkantak Hill in Chhattisgarh and merges with the Ganges River near Patna. The barrage itself is an impressive 1,407 metres (4,616 ft) long, making it the fourth longest barrage globally. It attracts a large number of tourists and visitors every year who are drawn to its scenic beauty and tranquil environment.
