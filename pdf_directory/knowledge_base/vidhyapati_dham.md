OVERVIEW:
Vidhyapati Dham in Samastipur district is a famous religious and literary pilgrimage site. It is the place of Nirvana of the renowned poet Mahakavi Vidyapati. The site houses the prominent Baleshwar Mahadev Temple, famous as Kamna Linga. It is one of the most worshiped shrines of Devadhidev in North Bihar, drawing numerous devotees, especially during the holy month of Sawan.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located at a distance of 38 kilometers from the Samastipur district headquarters, Vidyapati Dham is primarily centered around the Baleshwar Mahadev Temple. This temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva and is highly revered as Kamna Linga. The site holds a dual significance as both a spiritual destination and the place of Nirvana for the legendary poet Vidyapati. 

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The site is of immense cultural and historical importance as it marks the place of Nirvana of Mahakavi Vidyapati, one of the greatest poets of Maithili literature. Religiously, the Baleshwar Mahadev Temple is considered the most worshiped Devadhidev temple in North Bihar after Baba Baidhnath of Deoghar.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The temple is famous as Kamna Linga, where devotees believe their wishes are fulfilled. A large number of devotees gather to perform Jalabhishek (offering of sacred water) to the Shiva Linga during the holy month of Sawan.
