OVERVIEW:
The Ashoka Pillar in Vaishali is a prominent historical monument and the most popular sightseeing destination in the area. Situated near a Buddhist monastery and a coronation tank named Abhishek Pushkarni, this pillar is uniquely distinguished by its single lion capital.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Ashoka Pillar is a remarkable ancient structure located in Vaishali. It stands near a Buddhist monastery and the Abhishek Pushkarni, a coronation tank. While it shares the characteristic Lion Pillar design with other Ashokan pillars, this specific monument is distinct because it features only one lion capital at the top, making it a unique piece of Mauryan architecture.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It is an important archaeological and historical remnant of the Mauryan Empire, symbolizing the spread of Buddhism and ancient royal coronation practices associated with the nearby Abhishek Pushkarni.
