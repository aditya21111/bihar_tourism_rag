OVERVIEW:
Ajgaibinath Temple is a famous and ancient Lord Shiva temple situated on top of a hill in the bed of the River Ganga at Sultanganj, Bhagalpur. Known for its location in the Uttarvaahini Ganges (northward-flowing Ganges), the site is considered highly auspicious and attracts millions of pilgrims every year.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Ajgaibinath Temple is beautifully perched on a hill situated in the streams (watercourse) of the river Ganges in Sultanganj. The river here flows northward, referred to as Uttarvaahini Ganges, adding profound religious value to the location. The temple attracts millions of pilgrims, particularly those who gather to collect sacred Ganga Jal.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The temple holds immense significance as an ancient site dedicated to Lord Shiva. It is also the holy place where Jahnu Sage lived. The location's religious importance is amplified by the northward flow of the Ganges.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to tradition, this is the holy place where the legendary Jahnu Sage lived. Additionally, there is a deep-rooted tradition where every year pilgrims take the sacred Ganges water (Ganga Jal) from Sultanganj and carry it on foot to pray to Lord Shiva in Deoghar at the Babadham Temple.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
* Ancient temple of Shiva on top of a hill at the bed of the River Ganga.
* Holy place where Jahnu Sage lived.
* Location of the auspicious Uttarvaahini (northward flowing) Ganges.
* Starting point for pilgrims carrying Sacred Ganga Jal to Deoghar (Babadham Temple).
