OVERVIEW:
Telhar Waterfall is a mesmerizing natural gem situated near the Durgavati River in Kaimur. Surrounded by amazing views and featuring the Karamchat Dam, it is an ideal spot for picnics with family and friends.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Telhar Waterfall offers mesmerizing and soothing natural views, making it an amazing gem for tourists. Situated near the Durgavati River, the waterfall's ecosystem includes the adjacent 'Karamchat Dam'. Surrounded by magnificent scenery on all sides, the location serves as a perfect destination for picnics and family outings.
