OVERVIEW:
Maner Sharif is a historic center of learning and a revered Islamic pilgrimage site in Patna. It houses two beautifully crafted Mohammedan tombs, Chhoti Dargah and Bari Dargah, showcasing exquisite architectural nuances from the era of Jehangir.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Historically recognized as a prominent center of learning in early ages, Maner Sharif is now famous for its stunning architectural heritage. The site contains two well-known Mohammedan tombs: the tomb of Shah Daulat or Makhdum Daulat, known as Chhoti Dargah, and the tomb of Sheikh Yahia Maneri or Makhdum Yahia, known as Bari Dargah. The walls of Maner Sharif are adorned with high-finish carvings, crowned by a great dome, and the ceiling features beautifully carved inscriptions from the Quran. The intricate architectural details reflect the artistic brilliance of Jehangir's times.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It holds deep historical significance as an early center of learning and religious importance as a Dargah. Architecturally, it is an outstanding testament to the design and craftsmanship of the Mughal era, specifically during Jehangir's reign.
