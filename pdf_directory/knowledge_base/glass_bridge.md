OVERVIEW:
The Glass Bridge in Rajgir is a modern architectural marvel that adds a thrilling dimension to the city's attractions. Suspended over the lush landscapes, this transparent walkway provides panoramic views of the surrounding hills and valleys. Adventure enthusiasts and tourists can experience the sensation of walking on air while appreciating the natural beauty that Rajgir has to offer.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
A modern, transparent glass walkway suspended over a deep valley. The bridge offers an unobstructed, panoramic view of Rajgir's lush natural landscapes, hills, and valleys. It is designed to give visitors the thrilling sensation of walking mid-air.
