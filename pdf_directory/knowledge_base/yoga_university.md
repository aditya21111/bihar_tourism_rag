OVERVIEW:
The Yoga University, officially known as the Bihar School of Yoga, is located at Ganga Darshan Vishwa Yogapeeth in Munger. It serves as the karma bhumi of Sri Satyananda Saraswati and continues to teach the ancient wisdom of yoga for the benefit of people across the globe, aiming to bring simplicity, harmony, and balance into modern life.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Bihar School of Yoga is a globally recognized institution for yogic studies. It maintains ancient traditions and practices through the teachings of the Bihar School of Yoga, providing practical techniques and methods. The eternal flame, the "akhand jyoti", was lit over fifty years ago in Munger and continues to burn bright in Jyoti Mandir, symbolizing the fulfillment of Swami Satyananda's prophecy that 'Yoga will become the culture of tomorrow.'

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It is the karma bhumi of Sri Satyananda Saraswati. In Munger, Sri Swami Satyananda had the revelation to fulfill the mandate of his guru, Sri Swami Sivananda Saraswati, by creating a fellowship of yoga and devoting himself to the elevation of human consciousness by disseminating the light of yoga.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The "akhand jyoti" (eternal flame) has been burning continuously in Jyoti Mandir for over fifty years, representing the enduring light of yoga and the vision of its founders.
