OVERVIEW:
Kusheshwar Asthan Bird Wildlife Sanctuary spreads over 7019.75 acres in Darbhanga, comprising 14 waterlogged villages. It is a prime destination to spot endangered migratory birds from Mongolia and Siberia during the winter months.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Spreading over an expansive area of 7019.75 acres, the Kusheshwar Asthan Bird Sanctuary is essentially a unification of 14 waterlogged villages situated in the Kusheshwar Asthan block. The sanctuary transforms into a bird watcher's paradise between November and March. Visitors can spot endangered species of migratory birds from Mongolia and Siberia, along with indigenous species such as the Dalmatian Pelican, Indian Darter, Bar-headed goose, and many more.
