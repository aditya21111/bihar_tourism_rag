OVERVIEW:
Garhi Dam is situated on the Swarnarekha river in the Khaira block of Jamui. Constructed in 1978, it serves as a major source of irrigation and drinking water and has become a famous tourist spot for boating.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located on the Swarnarekha river in the Khaira block of the Jamui district, Garhi Dam was constructed by the Government of Bihar in 1978. While it primarily serves as a major source of water for irrigation and drinking purposes in the region, its scenic reservoir has made it a famous tourist spot, particularly known for boating activities.
