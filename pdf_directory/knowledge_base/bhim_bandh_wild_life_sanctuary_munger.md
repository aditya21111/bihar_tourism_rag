OVERVIEW:
Bhim Bandh Wildlife Sanctuary is beautifully situated south of the Ganges River, resting at the northern edge of the Chota Nagpur Plateau and west of Santhal Pargana. The sanctuary is renowned for its natural beauty, particularly the hot springs located in its valley portions and foothills. Among these, the finest are at Bhim Bandh, Sita Kund, and Rishi Kund. A unique feature of all these hot springs is that they maintain nearly the same temperature year-round. The Bhim Bandh spring itself boasts the hottest temperature, ranging from 52°C to 65°C, along with significant discharge. Surrounding the sanctuary are numerous places of tourist interest, including scenic lakes and other sacred kunds.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Bhim Bandh Wildlife Sanctuary offers an excellent blend of wildlife exploration and natural geological wonders. Situated along the geographical boundary of the Chota Nagpur Plateau, its landscape is characterized by valleys and foothills that house spectacular natural hot springs. These thermal springs are a major draw for tourists; notably, the Bhim Bandh hot spring is the hottest in the area with year-round temperatures between 52°C and 65°C. Other famous hot springs nearby include Sita Kund and Rishi Kund. Visitors to the sanctuary not only enjoy the rich biodiversity but also the surrounding tourist attractions like Rameshwar Kund and Kharagpur Lake, making it a comprehensive nature retreat.
