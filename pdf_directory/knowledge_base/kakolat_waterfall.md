OVERVIEW:
Kakolat Waterfall is a picturesque and popular picnic spot located in the Nawada district of Bihar. It features a beautiful natural waterfall that cascades down from a considerable height, forming a tranquil natural reservoir at its base, surrounded by a lush green environment.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Kakolat Waterfall cascades down from an impressive height ranging between 150 and 160 feet. The continuous flow of water drops into a wide, natural reservoir at the base, creating an ideal setting for tourists and locals to enjoy the natural beauty. The serene, forested surroundings add to the charm, making it a perfect retreat for nature lovers and families seeking a picturesque picnic spot.
