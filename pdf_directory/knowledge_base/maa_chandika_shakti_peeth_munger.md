OVERVIEW:
Chandika Sthan is a revered Hindu temple situated in Munger. It holds the prestigious status of being one of the fifty-one Shakti Peethas, which are places of worship consecrated to the goddess Shakti. Located on the northeast corner of Munger, just two kilometers away from the town center, it is considered a Siddhi-Peetha. Consequently, Chandika Sthan is regarded as one of the most sacred and sanctified temples in the region. According to legend, it was here that the left eye of Sati fell when Lord Shiva wandered the world carrying her corpse.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Chandika Sthan is an immensely sacred pilgrimage destination located just outside Munger town. As a Siddhi-Peetha and one of the 51 designated Shakti Peethas in Hinduism, the temple draws countless devotees who come to venerate the Divine Mother Chandi. The spiritual ambiance is enhanced by the profound mythological background linking the site to the mourning of Lord Shiva and the bodily remnants of Goddess Sati. The temple complex is a focal point of intense devotion and is essential to the spiritual geography of Bihar.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Extremely significant in Hindu mythology as one of the 51 Shakti Peethas, a central site for the worship of Goddess Shakti.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to legendary tales from Hindu folklore, to save the world from the destructive anger of Lord Shiva, who was carrying the corpse of Sati and wandering the earth, her body was dismembered. It is believed that the left eye of Sati fell at this exact location in Munger. The spot subsequently developed into a highly revered place of worship dedicated to the Divine Mother Chandi.
