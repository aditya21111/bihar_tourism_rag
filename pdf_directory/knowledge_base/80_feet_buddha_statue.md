OVERVIEW:
The 80 feet Buddha statue, also known as the Giant Buddha Statue, is a prominent stop in the Buddhist pilgrimage and tourist routes in Bodhgaya. Standing as a symbol of peace and spirituality of Buddhism, it is one of the largest statues in India. It features Lord Buddha in a meditation pose seated on a lotus in the open air.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The statue is 80 ft. high and depicts Lord Buddha in a meditation pose or dhyan mudra. He is seated on a lotus in the open air. The structure is built using a mix of sandstone blocks and red granite. It is an impressive piece of monumental art and is undoubtedly one of the largest statues in India.
