OVERVIEW:
Takhat Sri Harimandir ji is a magnificent Gurudwara situated in Patna City (Patna Sahib). It was built by Maharaja Ranjit Singh to commemorate the birthplace of the 10th Guru of Sikhs, Shri Guru Gobind Singh ji Maharaj. It stands as one of the five Takhats or Holy Seats of the Authority of the Sikhs, holding tremendous religious importance.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The magnificent Gurudwara features a very beautiful look, and visitors can deeply feel the power of people's faith the moment they enter. It serves as a monumental tribute to the 10th Sikh Guru. Several relics of Guru Govind Singh ji are preserved here. A Public kitchen or Langar serves food to all visitors round the year. 

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It holds immense religious significance as one of the five Takhats (Holy Seats of the Authority of the Sikhs). It commemorates the birthplace of the 10th Sikh Guru, Shri Guru Gobind Singh ji Maharaj, who was born on 22nd December 1666. The structure was built by Maharaja Ranjit Singh.
