OVERVIEW:
Basokund Jain Mandir in Vaishali is a highly revered pilgrimage site for Jainism, known as the birthplace of Lord Mahavir. The history of this religiously significant district extends from Lord Buddha to Lord Mahavir. Bhagwan Mahavir (Vardhamaan) was born here in 599 BC on 'chaitra shukla trayodashi' to King Siddhartha and Queen Trishala. The site houses the Bhagwan Mahaveer Janam Smarak and Bawan Pokhar Jain Temple, celebrated for their historical and religious value.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Basokund is recognized as the birthplace of Lord Mahavir, the 24th Tirthankara of Jainism. The site features the Bhagwan Mahaveer Janam Smarak, a temple and holy place dedicated to Jainism. Another significant structure here is the Bawan Pokhar Jain Temple, which historians believe was constructed by the Pallava kings. The area also hosts the Mahavir Center for the research of Jainism, principles of Ahimsa, and the study of the Prakrut language.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Vaishali district holds profound religious significance with its history entwined with both Lord Buddha and Lord Mahavir. Basokund specifically marks the birthplace of Bhagwan Mahavir in 599 BC. In 1956, Dr. Rajendra Prasad, the first President of India, visited the site to lay the foundation stone for the Mahavir Center to promote research in Jainism, Ahimsa, and the Prakrut language. A grand ceremony was held here to commemorate the 2600th birth anniversary of Lord Mahavir. Bawan Pokhar Jain Temple is believed to have been built by the Pallava kings.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to tradition, Vardhamaan (Lord Mahavir) was born to King Siddhartha and Queen Trishala in 599 BC on the auspicious day of 'chaitra shukla trayodashi' at this very location.
