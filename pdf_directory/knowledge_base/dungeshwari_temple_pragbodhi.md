OVERVIEW:
Dungeshwari Temple and the surrounding caves, located in the Dungeshwari Hills (Pragbodhi) along the Falgu river, are deeply intertwined with the spiritual journey of Lord Buddha. Though the caves may lack conventional visual appeal, they are renowned for offering a surreal experience and a sense of transcendental energy. It is believed that these caves sheltered Lord Buddha for six years before he travelled to Bodh Gaya to attain enlightenment.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located about 12-15 kms from Gaya along the Falgu river, the Dungeshwari Hills are home to a series of caves that now house several temples. The physical appearance of the caves is modest, but they are highly significant for their spiritual atmosphere. One of the primary cave temples in this area is dedicated to the Hindu goddess, Dungeshwari. The site is widely visited by pilgrims who are following the trail of Lord Buddha's historical journey.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The site is highly significant in Buddhist history and pilgrimage, as it represents the period of extreme asceticism practiced by Lord Buddha before he moved to Bodh Gaya. The Chinese pilgrim Hiuen Tsang historically mentioned these hills in his travelogue, adding to their archaeological and historical value.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to tradition, the caves in Dungeshwari Hills protected Lord Buddha for six years before he descended to the sacred town of Bodhgaya to attain enlightenment. It is believed that the transcendental energy of his meditation still lingers, offering a surreal experience to visitors.

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
Dungeshwari Temple, also known as Pragbodhi, is located in the Dungeshwari Hills about 12-15 kms from Gaya along the Falgu river. It is famous for the caves that protected Lord Buddha for six years prior to his enlightenment in Bodhgaya. The site also includes a temple dedicated to the Hindu goddess Dungeshwari and was mentioned by the ancient Chinese pilgrim Hiuen Tsang. Open from 05:00 AM to 05:00 PM, it is widely visited by pilgrims. The site is 19 km from Gaya International Airport and offers basic facilities like parking and drinking water. Nearby attractions include Mahabodhi Mandir (13 Kms) and Vishnupad Mandir (12 Kms).
