OVERVIEW:
Vishwa Shanti Stupa in Vaishali is a 125-foot tall peace pagoda built by the Buddh Vihar society in collaboration with the Japanese government in 1969. It is a beautiful white stupa surrounded by lush greenery, offering serenity and a pond where visitors can enjoy leisure boating. It stands near the sacred Abhishek Pushkarni tank.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The magnificent white stupa reaches a height of 125 feet and stands as a symbol of peace. The surroundings feature lush greenery and a pond offering leisure boating experiences. Next to the Vishwa Shanti Stupa lies Abhishek Pushkarni, also known as the coronation tank. During the time of the Lichchavis, the sacred waters of this tank were used to anoint elected representatives before they took their pledge.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Built in 1969 by the Buddh Vihar society in collaboration with the Japanese government, the stupa holds high cultural and religious significance as a symbol of global peace. The adjacent coronation tank, Abhishek Pushkarni, holds historical value from the time of the Lichchavis ancient republic.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The waters of the nearby Abhishek Pushkarni (coronation tank) were considered sacred and were traditionally used for anointing the elected representatives of the Lichchavis before their pledge.
