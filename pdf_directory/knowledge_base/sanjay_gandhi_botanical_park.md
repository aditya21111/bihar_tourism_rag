OVERVIEW:
Sanjay Gandhi Botanical Park, widely known as Patna Zoo, is the city's most frequented picnic spot. Initially established as a botanical garden in 1969, it transitioned into a biological park combining a botanical garden with a zoo in 1973. The zoo is currently home to over 800 animals of about 110 species, including tigers, leopards, clouded leopards, hippopotamuses, crocodiles, elephants, Himalayan black bears, jackals, black bucks, spotted deer, peafowl, hill myna, gharial, python, Indian rhinoceros, chimpanzees, giraffes, zebras, emus, and white peacocks. As a botanical garden, it houses more than 300 species of trees, herbs, and shrubs, with plant exhibits like a nursery for medicinal plants, an orchid house, a fern house, a glass house, and a rose garden. The park also includes a popular aquarium with about 35 species of fish, and a snake house with 32 snakes belonging to 5 species.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Sanjay Gandhi Botanical Park, commonly known as Patna Zoo, is a major wildlife and botanical attraction located on Bailey Road. Operating as both a zoo and a botanical garden since 1973, it is a haven for biodiversity in the city. The park spans a large area hosting over 800 animals across 110 species, notably the Indian rhinoceros, chimpanzee, giraffe, zebra, and various big cats. Furthermore, its botanical section boasts over 300 plant species, including specialized areas like the orchid house, fern house, glass house, rose garden, and medicinal plants nursery. It also features a snake house and a heavily visited aquarium, making it Patna's top picnic spot.
