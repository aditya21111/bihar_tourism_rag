OVERVIEW:
Jal Mandir is a highly sacred Jain temple situated in Pawapuri. It marks the exact holy site where Lord Mahavir, the last Tirthankar, attained 'Nirvana' and was subsequently buried.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Jal Mandir stands as a monument of immense veneration for the Jain community. Located in Pawapuri, approximately 90km away from Patna, the temple is built over the sacred burial site of Lord Mahavir, the 24th and last Tirthankar of Jainism. It was here that he attained 'Nirvana', making the temple a pivotal spiritual destination for pilgrims.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The site is of paramount religious importance as it is both the place of 'Nirvana' and the burial ground of Lord Mahavir, the last Tirthankar of Jainism.
