OVERVIEW:
Odhni Dam is a heavenly spot and an ideal location for amusement and relaxation in Banka. Surrounded by hills and filled with fresh water, it acts as a popular picnic area and tourist attraction. The site features a lovely garden and offers boating rides. The area near the Odhni Dam also serves as a biodiversity park close to Mandar hills.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The dam is built over the Odhani river, which is a tributary of the Chandan River in Banka. The fresh water dam is surrounded by scenic hills, making it a picturesque destination. It features a lovely garden area and facilities for boating rides in the dam's waters. Additionally, the vicinity serves as a biodiversity park near the famous Mandar hills, adding to its ecological value.
