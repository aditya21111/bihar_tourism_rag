OVERVIEW:
Sanjay Gandhi Botanical Park, commonly known as Patna Zoo, is a highly frequented picnic spot established as a botanical garden in 1969 and converted into a biological park in 1973. It uniquely combines a botanical garden with a zoo, housing over 800 animals of about 110 species, including tigers, leopards, elephants, and rhinos. The park also boasts an aquarium and a snake house, alongside more than 300 species of trees, herbs, and shrubs.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located in Patna, Sanjay Gandhi Botanical Park (Patna Zoo) is the city's most frequented picnic spot. It was initially established as a botanical garden in 1969 and transformed into a biological park combining a zoo and botanical garden in 1973. The zoo is currently home to over 800 animals belonging to about 110 species. Key wildlife species include the tiger, leopard, clouded leopard, hippopotamus, crocodile, elephants, Himalayan black bear, jackal, black bucks, spotted deer, peafowl, hill myna, gharial, python, Indian rhinoceros, chimpanzee, giraffe, zebra, emu, and white peacock. As a botanical garden, it houses over 300 species of trees, herbs, and shrubs. Plant exhibits include a medicinal plant nursery, an orchid house, a fern house, a glass house, and a rose garden. The park also features an aquarium with about 35 species of fish, which is the largest revenue generator after the general admission fee. A snake house within the premises has 32 snakes belonging to 5 species.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Established in 1969 and functioning as a biological park since 1973, it is an important conservation and recreational site in Bihar.
