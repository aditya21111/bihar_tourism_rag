OVERVIEW:
Kanwar Lake is Asia's largest freshwater oxbow lake, situated 22 km north-west of Begusarai Town in Manjhaul. Formed by the meandering of the Burhi Gandak river, it serves as an important habitat for migratory birds.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located 22 km north-west of Begusarai Town in Manjhaul, Kanwar Lake holds the distinction of being Asia's largest freshwater oxbow lake. The lake was naturally formed due to the meandering of the Burhi Gandak river, which is a tributary of the Ganga. The wetland ecosystem of the lake provides a highly important habitat for a large number of migratory birds.
