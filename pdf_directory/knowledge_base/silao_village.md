OVERVIEW:
Silao village is an ancient destination strategically located between Bihar Sarif and Rajgir. It is highly celebrated for its age-old tradition of making 'Khaja', making it a prominent culinary and cultural stop for tourists exploring the region.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Silao is deeply associated with the traditional culinary art of Khaja making. Khaja is a famous sweetmeat meticulously prepared using maida (wheat-flour), sugar, and ghee. Visitors to the village can find this delicacy in several varieties including Chandshahi, Round, Palvidar, and Gandhi Topa. Among these, the rectangular shaped Khaja is the most popular.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The village is known for its "ancient tradition" of Khaja making, preserving a historic culinary technique that continues to attract visitors.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The tradition of making Khaja has been passed down through generations, maintaining its status as a heritage culinary practice in Silao.
