OVERVIEW:
The Vishnupad Temple in Gaya is a highly sacred Hindu shrine dedicated to Lord Vishnu. Located on the banks of the Falgu River, it is revered for enshrining Lord Vishnu's footprint, believed to mark the exact spot where he stepped while pushing the demon Gayasura into the earth. The temple attracts flocks of pilgrims, particularly during the Pitru Paksha period, who come to perform vital rituals for their ancestors. With its intricate carvings and deep spiritual significance, the temple is a profoundly revered destination for devotees.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Vishnupad Temple is an ancient structure situated on the banks of the Falgu River in Gaya. It boasts beautiful architecture adorned with intricate carvings. The central feature of the shrine is the footprint of Lord Vishnu, enshrined within. This historical and sacred setting makes it a critical spiritual hub.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The temple holds immense spiritual significance in Hinduism, particularly for ancestral worship. The Pitru Paksha period sees massive gatherings of pilgrims performing rituals to honor and offer salvation to their ancestors.
