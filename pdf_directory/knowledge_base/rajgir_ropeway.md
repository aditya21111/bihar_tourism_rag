OVERVIEW:
The Rajgir Ropeway provides a scenic journey offering panoramic views of Rajgir's landscapes. This popular cable car experience connects the foothills to the Vishwa Shanti Stupa and the Ratnagiri Hills. Tourists enjoy the breathtaking vistas and a convenient mode of transport to these significant landmarks in Rajgir.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
An aerial cable car system that serves as a convenient mode of transport as well as a scenic attraction. It carries passengers from the foothills up to the Ratnagiri Hills and the Vishwa Shanti Stupa, providing panoramic and breathtaking views of the surrounding natural landscape.
