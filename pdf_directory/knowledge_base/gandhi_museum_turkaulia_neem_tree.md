OVERVIEW:
The Gandhi Museum and the Turkaulia Neem Tree are historically significant sites located in Turkaulia Block in Motihari, East Champaran. The Gandhi Museum was laid down on 10th June 1972 by Governor Mr. D K Barooch. The nearby neem tree in Turkaulia block is a witness to the unfolding of great movements in history, specifically tied to the Champaran Movement.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This historic attraction encompasses both the Gandhi Museum and the Turkaulia Neem Tree. The Gandhi Museum, inaugurated in 1972, stands as a tribute to Mahatma Gandhi's involvement in the region. The Turkaulia Neem Tree is a living historical monument that bears witness to the great events and movements that unfolded during the Champaran Movement, symbolizing the struggle for independence.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The site is intrinsically linked to the Champaran Movement and the Indian independence struggle. The Gandhi Museum was established to commemorate this legacy. The Turkaulia Neem Tree is an important historical relic that stood witness to these momentous events.
