Sanjay Gandhi Biological Park, widely known as Patna Zoo, is a renowned zoological park in Patna. Home to rare and endangered species, it offers immersive experiences with safari tours, a botanical garden, aquarium, and butterfly house.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Sanjay Gandhi Biological Park, also recognized as Patna Zoo, is a highly renowned zoological park situated in the capital city of Patna. The park provides sanctuary to a diverse range of flora and fauna, including numerous rare and endangered species. To provide visitors an immersive and educational experience, the park offers multiple attractions including safari tours, a well-maintained botanical garden, an aquarium, and a butterfly house, making it a must-visit destination for nature enthusiasts.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The park is named after Sanjay Gandhi, the son of the former Prime Minister of India, Indira Gandhi.
