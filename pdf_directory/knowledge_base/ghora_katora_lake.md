OVERVIEW:
Ghora Katora Lake is a beautiful and serene spot located in Rajgir. Nestled in a breathtaking valley and situated near the World Peace Pagoda, the lake offers a spectacular view from the hilltops. Known for its connection to the Indian epic Mahabharata, it is a peaceful destination that features a glorious sandstone statue of Buddha situated right in the middle of the lake.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Ghora Katora Lake is surrounded by a breathtaking valley and offers visitors serene natural beauty. From the hilltop, the wide spread of the lake and the valley provide a spectacular view. In the middle of the lake on a lotus pedestal, there is a glorious sandstone statue of Buddha. The lake also offers boating facilities for the merriment of visitors. It is located near the World Peace Pagoda.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The lake has deep roots in Hindu mythology. According to the epic Mahabharata, King Jarasandha had his stable at this very location, which is why it is named Ghora Katora.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to Hindu mythology and the Indian epic Mahabharata, King Jarasandha maintained his stable here, leading to the name Ghora Katora (which translates to 'Horse Bowl').
