OVERVIEW:
The Ropeway at Mandar Hill in Banka, newly constructed by the Tourism Department, offers a highly convenient and scenic way to reach the mountain peak. It dramatically reduces the 1-hour physical climb to a pleasant 4-minute ride, leading to increased tourist footfall. Tourists can easily access the historical, mythological, and religious places situated on Mandar Hill while enjoying panoramic views of the valleys below.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Mandar Hill Ropeway is a modern infrastructure project constructed by the Tourism Department. It carries tourists from the mountain lowlands directly to the peak in just 4 minutes. The ropeway allows visitors to avoid an hour-long climb and provides a pleasant aerial experience with panoramic valley views. From the top, tourists have easy access to important religious points such as Sita Kund, Lord Narsingh Cave Temple, Goshala, and Yoni Kund.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The ropeway provides essential access to the culturally and religiously significant Mandar Hill, allowing tourists to effortlessly visit various mythological landmarks situated on the mountain.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
Mandar Hill is steeped in mythology and is home to multiple important religious sites like Sita Kund, Lord Narsingh Cave Temple, Goshala, and Yoni Kund.
