OVERVIEW:
Bodh Gaya, nestled by the Falgu River, stands as a spiritual sanctuary where Gautama Buddha attained enlightenment. DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Mahabodhi Temple is nestled by the Falgu River. It is a spiritual sanctuary and the focal point of pilgrimage in Bodh Gaya. It houses the Bodhi Tree. The temple's architecture is adorned with intricate carvings, which creates a sacred ambiance, inviting reflection. Bodh Gaya and the Mahabodhi Temple together offer a profound journey into Buddhist spirituality.
