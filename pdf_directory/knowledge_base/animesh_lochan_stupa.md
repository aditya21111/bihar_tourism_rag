OVERVIEW:
Animesh Lochan Stupa is a significant sacred monument within the Mahabodhi Temple complex in Bodhgaya. It marks the spot where Lord Buddha spent the second week after attaining enlightenment, gazing at the Bodhi tree in deep gratitude and divine contentment without blinking his eyes.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This stupa stands as a testament to the period immediately following Buddha's enlightenment. It is built at the exact site where Buddha spent a week in unblinking meditation, expressing his profound gratitude towards the Bodhi tree for providing him shelter during his spiritual quest. 

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The stupa underscores the themes of gratitude, peace, and deep spiritual absorption in Buddhism. It forms an integral part of the pilgrim circuit in Bodhgaya detailing the seven weeks Buddha spent following his enlightenment.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to tradition, in the 2nd week after attaining enlightenment, Buddha stood at this spot and continued to gaze at the great Bodhi tree without blinking his eyes, fully absorbed in divine contentment.
