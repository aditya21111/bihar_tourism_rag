OVERVIEW:
The Vishwa Shanti Stupa in Vaishali is a modern architectural addition to the ancient landscape. Built by the Buddha Vihar Society, it is situated on the southern side of the Abhishek Pushkarni Lake (Coronation Tank). The newly built stupa serves as a symbol of world peace and spiritual reflection in the historic region of Vaishali.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Positioned peacefully by the holy waters of the Coronation Tank, the Vishwa Shanti Stupa is a contemporary monument built by the Buddha Vihar Society. It stands as a beacon of peace in an area steeped in early democratic and Buddhist history, complementing the ancient stupas and archaeological ruins found throughout Vaishali.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Though a newly built structure, it aligns with Vaishali's profound Buddhist heritage, honoring Lord Buddha's multiple visits to the region, his last sermon at Kolhua, and the 2nd great Buddhist council hosted here.
