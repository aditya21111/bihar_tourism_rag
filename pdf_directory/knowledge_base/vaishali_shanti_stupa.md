OVERVIEW:
The Vaishali Shanti Stupa, also known as Vishwa Shanti Stupa or World Peace Pagoda, is an important monument built by the Nipponzan Myohoji sect of Japan in Vaishali. Vaishali is historically significant as the place where Lord Buddha preached his last sermon before his Nirvana. The stupa is erected to disseminate love and peace on earth according to the teachings of the Saddharma Pundarika Sutra (The Lotus Sutra).

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Vishwa Shanti Stupa is a World Peace Pagoda erected in Vaishali. It was built according to the teachings of the Saddharma Pundarika Sutra (The Lotus Sutra) to spread love and peace and to create 'The Pure Land' on earth. The stupa was built by the Nipponzan Myohoji sect of Japan.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Vaishali holds immense religious significance as the place where Buddha preached his last sermon before attaining Nirvana. The stupa itself is part of a global initiative of constructing peace pagodas initiated by Ven. Nichidatsu Fuji Guruji after witnessing the tragedy of the atomic bombings in Hiroshima and Nagasaki at the end of World War II.
