OVERVIEW:
Valmiki Tiger Reserve forms the easternmost limit of the Himalayan Terai forests in India and is the only tiger reserve in Bihar. Situated in the Gangetic Plains bio-geographic zone, the forest is a beautiful combination of Bhabar and Terai tracts. Known for Tigers, Bisons, and various wild animals in their natural habitat, it offers thrilling experiences like Tiger Safari, white water rafting, and hanging bridges, making it a picture-perfect tourist destination.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located in Valmiki Nagar, Bagaha, the reserve features a diverse ecosystem with a mix of Bhabar and Terai tracts. It is part of the Gangetic Plains bio-geographic zone and serves as a vital habitat for majestic wildlife, particularly Tigers and Bisons. The lush environment is full of Champa trees, giving the district its name. Tourists can indulge in adventurous activities such as the Tiger Safari, exploring hanging bridges, and participating in white water rafting. Additional facilities like a watch tower, Golghar, boat safari, and cycling make the visit highly engaging.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The name of the district is derived from two words: 'Champa' and 'Aranya', meaning the 'Forest of Champa trees'. It acts as an important ecological zone as the only tiger reserve in Bihar and the easternmost limit of India's Himalayan Terai forests.
