ALTERNATIVE NAMES:

LOCATION:
Dungeshwari Hills, near Bodhgaya

DISTRICT / REGION:
Gaya

CATEGORY:
Religious Site / Cave Temple

OVERVIEW:
Dungeshwari Hill Cave, also known as Pragbodhi Mountain, is a deeply revered pilgrimage site in the Gaya district. It is the sacred location where Gautama Buddha is believed to have meditated for a long period before moving to Bodhgaya to attain enlightenment. The site features multiple cave temples enshrining statues of Buddha and a Hindu goddess.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Dungeshwari Hill Cave comprises several cave temples connected by a concreted walkway. Inside one of the cave temples, a striking golden bony Buddha statue is enshrined, symbolizing his severe asceticism before enlightenment. In another cave, a tall statue of the Buddha is placed. Additionally, a Hindu goddess, Dungeshwari, is enshrined inside the cave temple, showcasing the site's spiritual importance for both Buddhists and Hindus.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
This mountain is known historically as Pragbodhi (meaning "prior to enlightenment") because it represents the crucial phase of extreme austerity and meditation Gautama underwent just before he journeyed to Bodhgaya to achieve enlightenment under the Bodhi Tree.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
It is a widespread belief that Gautama meditated in these very caves for a long period, practicing extreme asceticism, which is depicted by the bony golden statue enshrined here.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
* Golden bony Buddha statue.
* Tall Buddha statue in an adjacent cave.
* Shrine of Hindu Goddess Dungeshwari.
* Concreted walkway leading to the temples.
