OVERVIEW:
Rajgir Wildlife Safari is a premier wildlife destination located in Rajgir, Bihar, developed by the Department of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Govt of Bihar. Spread over an expansive 191 hectares, the zoo safari is designed to accommodate over 250 herbivores and carnivores. The destination provides tourists an immersive nature and wildlife experience with different safari zones dedicated to various animal species.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The zoo safari spans 191 hectares and is divided into five distinct zones: Herbivore Safari, Bear Safari, Leopard Safari, Tiger Safari, and Lion Safari. It houses a diverse collection of over 250 wild animals. Species thriving here include Chital, Sambar, Black Buck, Hog Deer, Barking Deer, Wild Boar, Sloth Bear, Indian Leopard, Royal Bengal Tiger, and Asiatic Lion. An added attraction for visitors is a specialized selfie point featuring life-size statues of wild animals.

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
Nature Safari & Zoo Safari, located in Rajgir, Nalanda, Bihar, is a prominent wildlife area spread over 191 hectares. Developed by the Dept. of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Govt of Bihar, the safari features five distinct zones: Herbivore, Bear, Leopard, Tiger, and Lion Safaris. It houses over 250 animals, including Royal Bengal Tigers and Asiatic Lions. Key facilities include parking, restaurants, and a souvenir shop. It is situated 105 kms from Patna Airport and 6 kms from Rajgir Railway Station. Visitors can also explore nearby attractions like the New Ropeway, Cyclopean Wall, and Griddhakuta. Entry fee is Rs. 250 and it is best visited from October to March.
