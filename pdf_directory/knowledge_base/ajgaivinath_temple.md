OVERVIEW:
Ajgaivinath Temple is a famous Lord Shiva temple situated in the streams (watercourse) of the river Ganges in Sultanganj, Bhagalpur. This holy place is where Jahnu Sage lived and the river Ganges flows northward (Uttarvaahini Ganges), which is considered highly auspicious. The ancient temple of Shiva on top of a hill at the bed of River Ganga attracts millions of pilgrims, particularly those collecting Sacred Ganga Jal on their way to pray at Deoghar (Babadham Temple).

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The ancient temple of Shiva is prominently situated on top of a hill right at the bed of the River Ganga. It lies in the streams (watercourse) of the river Ganges at Sultanganj. The location is characterized by the Ganges flowing northward, known as Uttarvaahini Ganges, making it a sacred site. The temple serves as a vital pilgrimage stop where millions of devotees take sacred water from the Ganges to carry on their spiritual journey to Deoghar.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It is an ancient and holy place associated with Jahnu Sage. The northward flow of the Ganges (Uttarvaahini) here is considered highly auspicious. It plays a critical role in regional pilgrimage circuits, particularly for devotees traveling to Babadham Temple in Deoghar.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to tradition, Jahnu Sage lived at this holy place. Every year, pilgrims collect sacred water (Sacred Ganga Jal) from Sultanganj before proceeding to offer prayers to Lord Shiva in Deoghar (Babadham Temple).

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
Ajgaivinath Temple is an ancient and highly revered Lord Shiva temple located on a hill in the streams of the Ganges River at Sultanganj, Bhagalpur, Bihar. Known for its auspicious northward-flowing Uttarvaahini Ganges, the site is associated with Jahnu Sage. It acts as a major religious hub where millions of pilgrims collect sacred Ganga Jal on their way to Babadham Temple in Deoghar. The site is best visited between October and March. It is well-connected, with Sultanganj Railway Station and Bus Stand just 1 km away. The nearby attraction is the Vikramshila Dolphin Sanctuary. Various travel operators, guides, and hotels provide support to tourists.
