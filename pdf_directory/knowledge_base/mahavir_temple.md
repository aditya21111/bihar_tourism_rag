OVERVIEW:
Mahavir Mandir in Patna is one of the holiest Hindu temples dedicated to Lord Hanuman. It is a highly revered spiritual destination that attracts millions of pilgrims every year. Known for its grand architecture and deep religious significance, the temple is a focal point of worship and celebration in the city.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located prominently in Patna, the Mahavir Temple features striking traditional Hindu temple architecture with its distinct multi-tiered red shikharas. The temple is dedicated to Lord Hanuman. It experiences immense footfall, particularly on Saturdays and Tuesdays, which are the traditional worshipping days of Lord Hanuman, often resulting in long, winding queues of devotees. The temple complex is well-organized to handle large crowds, especially during major religious festivals.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It stands as one of the holiest Hindu shrines dedicated to Lord Hanuman in the region, deeply ingrained in the local culture and the spiritual life of Patna's residents and visitors.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
Saturdays and Tuesdays are marked as traditional worshipping days for Lord Hanuman, drawing massive crowds of devotees seeking blessings.
