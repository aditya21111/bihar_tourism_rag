OVERVIEW:
Chhath Puja is a significant Hindu festival celebrated in Bihar and across India to honor the Sun God. This four-day festival involves rigorous rituals, including fasting, bathing in rivers, and offering prayers to the rising and setting sun. It is a time of reflection, gratitude, and devotion that fosters a sense of community and embodies the cultural richness of the region.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
A major four-day religious festival deeply embedded in Bihar's traditions. The festival honors the Sun God and involves rigorous rituals performed near water bodies. Devotees fast, bathe in rivers, and make specific offerings to the sun both at dawn and dusk. The event highlights a reverence for nature and fosters deep communal ties.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It holds immense spiritual and cultural significance in Bihar, representing gratitude towards the Sun God for sustaining life on earth. It also beautifully embodies a reverence for nature.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
Involves traditions of fasting, river bathing, and offering prayers and gratitude to both the rising and setting sun.
