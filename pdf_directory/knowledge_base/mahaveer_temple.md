OVERVIEW:
Mahavir Mandir is one of the holiest Hindu temples dedicated to Lord Hanuman, located at Patna Junction, Patna. It is visited by millions of pilgrims every year, with long winding queues often seen on Saturdays and Tuesdays, which are the traditional worshiping days of Lord Hanuman. Thousands of devotees visit the Mahavir Mandir during Ram Navami and New Year celebrations. During Ram Navami, waiting queues can stretch up to a kilometer long.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Mahavir Mandir is a prominent religious site dedicated to Lord Hanuman. The temple sees massive footfall, particularly on Saturdays, Tuesdays, Ram Navami, and New Year celebrations. The structure accommodates thousands of pilgrims, and it is a major landmark near Patna Junction.
