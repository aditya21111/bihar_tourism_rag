OVERVIEW:
The Mahabodhi Temple is a highly revered ancient, rebuilt, and restored Buddhist temple in Bodh Gaya, Bihar. Recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site, it marks the exact location where Gautama Buddha is said to have attained enlightenment. The temple complex is home to a descendant of the sacred Bodhi Tree and has been a major pilgrimage destination for Buddhists worldwide for over two thousand years.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located about 15 Kms from Gaya and 96 Kms from Patna, the Mahabodhi Temple stands as the most critical site in the Buddhist world. The site features an ancient but masterfully restored temple structure. The spiritual heart of the temple is the Bodhi Tree, under which the Buddha meditated and gained enlightenment; the site currently houses a direct descendant of that original tree. The destination holds a profound aura of peace and history, drawing pilgrims and tourists alike.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It holds unparalleled historical and spiritual significance as the very site of Buddha's enlightenment. It has been the center of Buddhist pilgrimage for over two millennia and is officially designated as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to Buddhist tradition, this is the exact spot where Siddhartha Gautama meditated under the Bodhi tree and attained enlightenment, becoming the Buddha.
