OVERVIEW:
Pineapple is grown in Kishanganj in an organic manner with farmers relying on age-old techniques to deal with pest attacks and diseases. Cow manure and cow urine are liberally used to enrich the soil, which gives Kishanganj pineapples a vibrant golden yellow colour, making them extremely sweet and juicy with a heady aroma. Local delicacies are also prepared using fresh pineapples from this district. 

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Pineapple Farms in Kishanganj offer a glimpse into organic farming practices using traditional age-old techniques. Farmers use natural fertilizers like cow manure and cow urine to enrich the soil, avoiding synthetic chemicals for pest and disease control. The farms produce highly sought-after pineapples characterized by their vibrant golden yellow colour, juicy texture, extreme sweetness, and heady aroma. Visitors can experience the scenic expanses of pineapple cultivation and explore the local delicacies prepared from fresh district pineapples.

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
The Pineapple Farms in Kishanganj, Bihar, are an agricultural tourism attraction known for their organic farming practices. Using age-old techniques, cow manure, and cow urine, the farmers produce pineapples with a distinct golden yellow color, intense sweetness, and a heady aroma. The site is open from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, best visited between October and March. Facilities include parking, restaurants, drinking water, and a souvenir shop, with photography allowed. The nearest airport is Bagdogra (33 Kms) and the nearest rail/bus stations are at Thakurganj & Pothia (14 Kms). Nearby attractions include Khajra Mela, Churhi Killa, Hargori Mandir, and Nehru Park.
