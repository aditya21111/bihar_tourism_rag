OVERVIEW:
Gol Ghar, a prominent landmark in Patna, Bihar, is an architectural marvel built by Captain John Garstin in 1786. This granary, resembling a beehive, was constructed to address famines. It stands as a testament to historical engineering and contributes to Patna's cultural heritage.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Gol Ghar is a prominent landmark and architectural marvel situated in Patna. It was designed as a large granary resembling a beehive shape. It was specifically constructed to store grain and address famines in the region. Today, it stands as an impressive testament to historical engineering and remains an important part of Patna's cultural heritage.
