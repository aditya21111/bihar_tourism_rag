OVERVIEW:
Ram Rekha Ghat, situated in Buxar, is a sacred river bank along the Ganges. It is highly revered by pilgrims and devotees who visit for spiritual cleansing and religious rituals. The ghat is celebrated for its deep historical and mythological roots, particularly its association with the epic Ramayana. Its serene ambiance makes it an inviting destination for those seeking a connection to Hindu mythology and the sanctity of the Ganges River.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Ram Rekha Ghat is a sacred riverbank situated along the Ganges in Buxar. The site offers a serene ambiance, drawing pilgrims and devotees who come for spiritual cleansing and to partake in various religious rituals by the holy river.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The ghat holds profound historical and religious significance as it is believed to be the exact spot where Lord Rama crossed the river Ganges while on his journey to Janakpur. Its strong association with the epic Ramayana makes it a deeply revered destination.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to Hindu mythology and the epic Ramayana, this is the location where Lord Rama crossed the Ganges river on his way to Janakpur. Devotees visit for spiritual cleansing and to perform religious rituals.
