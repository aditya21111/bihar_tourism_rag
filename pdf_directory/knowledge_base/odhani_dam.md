OVERVIEW:
Odhani Dam is a popular tourist destination in Banka, offering stunning panoramic views of the river and dam. It is an ideal recreational spot where visitors can enjoy water sports and picnicking in a tranquil atmosphere.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Odhani Dam offers stunning panoramic views combining the river and the dam structure. It has been developed into an adventure and recreational hub where tourists can partake in motorboat rides, speed boating, jet skiing, and other adventurous water sports. The lush greenery and serene surroundings make it an ideal place for picnicking, relaxing, and unwinding.
