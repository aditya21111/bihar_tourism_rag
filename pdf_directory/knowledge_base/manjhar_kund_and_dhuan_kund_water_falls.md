OVERVIEW:
Manjhar Kund and Dhuan Kund are major and highly scenic waterfalls located in the Kaimur hills, about 10 Kms from Sasaram. Formed by the Kav river, the waters flow through a zigzag path and cascade down the high mountain to form these beautiful water bodies within a 3-kilometer radius. The 36.5-meter high plunge of Dhuan Kund creates a mesmerizing fog-like mist, drawing tourists from all over Bihar and beyond for its soothing and spectacular natural beauty.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located within a radius of 3 kilometers in the picturesque Kaimur hills, the Manjhar Kund and Dhuan Kund waterfalls are prominent natural attractions in Bihar, situated about 10 Kms from Sasaram. The Kav river descends from the mountain, flowing through a winding path before dramatically falling from the high mountain to form the Manjhar Kund waterfall, presenting a highly soothing and beautiful sight. A short distance away is the Dhuan Kund waterfall, where water gushes down forcefully from a deep valley drop of 36.5 meters. The immense height and force of the falling water generate a thick mist or fog all around, which gave it the name "Dhuan Kund" (Smoke pool). The captivating beauty of these falls attracts a multitude of tourists not only from Rohtas but also from Kaimur, Aurangabad, Bhojpur, Patna, and other states.

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
Manjhar Kund and Dhuan Kund are majestic waterfalls situated in the Kaimur hills, about 10 kms from Sasaram in the Rohtas district. The Kav river cascades from the mountains, with Dhuan Kund falling from a dramatic height of 36.5 meters, creating a thick fog-like mist. The best time to visit these waterfalls is during the monsoon from July to September between 06:00 AM and 05:00 PM. They are located 12 kms from Sasaram Railway Station. Nearby attractions include Maa Tarachandi Dham, Sher Shah Suri Tomb, and Tutla Bhawani Waterfall.
