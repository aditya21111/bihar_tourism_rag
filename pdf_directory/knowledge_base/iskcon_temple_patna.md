OVERVIEW:
The ISKCON Temple in Patna, also known as Sri Radha Banke Bihari Ji Mandir, is a revered shrine dedicated to Lord Krishna. Located on Budh Marg, this magnificent 108-foot-tall multi-storey temple is an architectural marvel supported by 84 pillars and covers an expansive area of two acres. It is a major spiritual destination in the city.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located on Budh Marg in Patna, the ISKCON Temple is a major spiritual and cultural landmark dedicated to Lord Krishna. Known formally as Sri Radha Banke Bihari Ji Mandir, the temple features a grand multi-storey structure that stands 108 feet tall. Its architectural brilliance is highlighted by the 84 pillars that support the massive shrine, which spans across a two-acre complex. The temple offers a serene environment for devotion and spiritual gatherings.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It holds deep religious significance for the devotees of Lord Krishna and serves as an important center for the ISKCON community in Patna, promoting spiritual teachings, devotion, and cultural heritage.
