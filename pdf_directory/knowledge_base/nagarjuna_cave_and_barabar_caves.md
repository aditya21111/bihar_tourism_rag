ATTRACTION: Nagarjuna Cave & Barabar Caves

ALTERNATIVE NAMES:
Nagarjuni Caves

LOCATION:
Nagarjuna Hills, near Barabar Hills

DISTRICT / REGION:
Jehanabad

CATEGORY:
Historical Site / Heritage Site

OVERVIEW:
Nagarjuna Hills and Barabar Hills are located in the Jehanabad district of Bihar, about 35 Kms north of Gaya. This important archaeological site features a collection of caves with historical inscriptions. The Nagarjuni Caves are often grouped with the nearby Barabar Caves as a single monument complex. There are three specific caves excavated in the Nagarjuna Hills: the Vadathi-ka-Kubha, the Vapiya-ka-Kubha, and the Gopi-ka-Kubha.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The attraction comprises rock-cut caves carved into the Nagarjuna and Barabar hills. The Nagarjuna Hills are situated less than 2 Kms from the Barabar Hills. Within the Nagarjuna Hills, three distinct caves have been excavated: Vadathi-ka-Kubha, Vapiya-ka-Kubha, and Gopi-ka-Kubha. These caves feature historical inscriptions and are recognized as a highly important archaeological and heritage site.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The caves are a significant archaeological site holding historical inscriptions. They form an important collection of monuments reflecting ancient rock-cut architecture in Bihar.

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
Nagarjuna Cave & Barabar Caves form an important archaeological and historical site in Jehanabad district, Bihar. Located 35 Kms north of Gaya, the site features three main excavated caves in the Nagarjuna Hills (Vadathi-ka-Kubha, Vapiya-ka-Kubha, and Gopi-ka-Kubha) containing historical inscriptions. It is best visited between October and March. Nearby attractions include Sidheswar Nath Temple and Pataal Ganga. Nearest airport is Gaya (42 Kms).
