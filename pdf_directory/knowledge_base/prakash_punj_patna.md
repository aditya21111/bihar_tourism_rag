OVERVIEW:
Prakash Punj is a newly constructed park in Patna with state-of-the-art facilities, built near Guru Ka Bagh. The park honors the legacy of Sikh Gurus and features four monumental gates dedicated to the sons of Guru Gobind Singh. Additionally, the complex houses a museum that uses audio-video visuals and paintings to narrate the teachings and lives of all ten Sikh Gurus.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Prakash Punj is a modern, newly constructed park in the city of Patna, located near Guru Ka Bagh. The park features state-of-the-art facilities and profound Sikh cultural themes. A defining architectural feature of Prakash Punj are its four major gates, each named after the four sons of Guru Gobind Singh Maharaj: Ajit Singh, Fateh Singh, Jujhar Singh, and Zorawar Singh. Furthermore, these gates encompass five circular walls named Hemkund Sahib, Paonta Sahib, Nanded Sahib, Keshgarh Sahib, and Patna Sahib. Within the Prakash Punj complex, a museum has been built to highlight the life, teachings, and works of Guru Gobind Singh. This museum showcases graphic illustrations, audio-video visuals, and paintings detailing the lives of all ten Sikh Gurus for tourists and devotees.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Prakash Punj is a highly significant cultural monument for the Sikh faith in Patna. It commemorates the lives of all ten Sikh Gurus and pays special tribute to Guru Gobind Singh and his four sons.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The park honors the legacy of the Sikh Gurus and celebrates the historical ties between Patna and the Sikh faith.
