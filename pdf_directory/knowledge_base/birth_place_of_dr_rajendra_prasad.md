OVERVIEW:
The Birth Place of Dr. Rajendra Prasad is a significant historical and national heritage site located in Ziradei (also spelled Jiradei), in the Siwan district of Bihar. It is the ancestral home where Dr. Rajendra Prasad, the first President of India, an eminent politician, lawyer, journalist, and scholar, was born in a Kayastha family.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The site preserves the ancestral residence and birth location of Dr. Rajendra Prasad. The property features a majestic traditional Indian architectural style with white pillars and well-maintained green lawns, offering a glimpse into the early life and roots of one of India's most respected freedom fighters and the first President of independent India.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Dr. Rajendra Prasad (3 December 1884 – 28 February 1963) played a vital role in India's freedom struggle. Serving as the first President of India, his birthplace serves as an inspiration and educational site showcasing his legacy as a leader, journalist, lawyer, and scholar.
