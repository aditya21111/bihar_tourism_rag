OVERVIEW:
The Glass Bridge is a thrilling 200-foot transparent structure situated inside the Nature Safari Park in Rajgir, Nalanda. It offers visitors an exhilarating adventure alongside a range of other nature and adventure activities, providing stunning views of the surrounding natural landscape.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The magnificent 200-foot glass bridge is constructed using three robust layers of 15-mm glass, assuring both safety and an adrenaline-pumping experience. Built within the lush Nature Safari Park, the destination allows visitors to traverse the transparent walkway while taking in panoramic views of the dense forests and hills. In addition to the bridge, the park offers exciting adventure sports and activities like zip lining, air cycling, nature park safaris, and picnic spots.

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
The Glass Bridge in Nalanda is a popular natural and adventure attraction situated inside the Nature Safari Park in Rajgir, Bihar. The 200-foot bridge is made from three layers of 15-mm glass. The park also provides adventure activities like zip lining and air cycling. It is located 110 Kms from Patna Airport and 6 Kms from Rajgir Railway Station. Key nearby attractions include the Ropeway, Son Bhandar, and Venu Van. The site is open to visitors from 10:00 AM to 02:00 PM.
