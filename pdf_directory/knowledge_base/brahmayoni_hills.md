OVERVIEW:
Brahmayoni Hills is a prominent natural and historical landmark in Gaya, located just about 1 km from the famous Vishnupad Temple. It is a site of great Buddhist significance, where Lord Buddha stayed before continuing his journey to enlightenment.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
To reach the top of Brahmayoni Hills, visitors must ascend a flight of 424 steps. The summit offers spiritual solace and a connection to ancient history. Lord Buddha stayed here for some days before proceeding to Sujata Garh and the Dungeshwari Hills. In honor of this sacred association, Emperor Ashoka built a stupa over here to mark his commitment and devotion to Buddhism.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The hill marks an important stop in Lord Buddha's journey leading up to his enlightenment. The presence of a stupa built by Emperor Ashoka underlines its historical importance during the Mauryan era.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to traditions, Lord Buddha rested at this very location for a few days during his spiritual quest before moving onward to Dungeshwari Hills and eventually Bodhgaya.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
* A steep ascent of 424 steps to reach the top.
* Location where Lord Buddha stayed for some days.
* Remains of a stupa built by Emperor Ashoka.
* Close proximity to the Vishnupad Temple.
