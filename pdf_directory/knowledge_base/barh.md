OVERVIEW:
Barh is a small town located in the Patna district, situated between Bakhtiarpur and Mokama. It is renowned as a culinary destination for its traditional local sweet known as "Lai," attracting visitors who wish to explore the regional flavors of Bihar.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The town of Barh is uniquely famous for a traditional sweetmeat named Lai. This sweet is meticulously prepared in the shape of a ball or cake. The recipe includes 'lthobi' or 'Ramdana' seeds, 'Khoa', and sugar, making it a distinctive local delicacy and a hallmark of Barh's culinary culture.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The preparation of Lai is a significant culinary tradition of Barh, adding to the cultural richness of the Patna district.
