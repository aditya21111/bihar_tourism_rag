OVERVIEW:
Bihar School of Yoga, located at Ganga Darshan Vishwa Yogapeeth in Munger, is an esteemed institution and the karma bhumi of Sri Satyananda Saraswati. It is a prominent center teaching the ancient wisdom of yoga for the benefit of people worldwide. The institution maintains ancient traditions and provides practical techniques to bring simplicity, harmony, and balance into modern life.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Bihar School of Yoga is situated at Ganga Darshan Vishwa Yogapeeth in Munger. It is the site where Sri Swami Satyananda received the revelation to fulfill his guru Sri Swami Sivananda Saraswati's mandate by creating a fellowship of yoga. The center is dedicated to elevating human consciousness by disseminating the light of yoga. A significant feature is the akhand jyoti (eternal flame), which was lit over fifty years ago and continues to burn brightly in Jyoti Mandir, symbolizing the prophecy that 'Yoga will become the culture of tomorrow.' The school teaches ancient yoga traditions, practices, and teachings.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The institution serves as the karma bhumi of Sri Satyananda Saraswati. It holds immense spiritual and cultural importance as a global center for yoga education, carrying forward the teachings of Sri Swami Sivananda Saraswati and Sri Swami Satyananda.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
It was here that Sri Swami Satyananda had a revelation to create a fellowship of yoga. The 'akhand jyoti' (eternal flame) lit over fifty years ago continues to burn in the Jyoti Mandir, a testament to the prophecy that 'Yoga will become the culture of tomorrow.'
