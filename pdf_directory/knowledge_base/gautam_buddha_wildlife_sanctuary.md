CATEGORY:
Wildlife Area

OVERVIEW:
Constituted in 1976 on the line of Project Tiger, the Gautam Buddha Wildlife Sanctuary is located in the South-East part of Gaya. It is highly rich in terms of biodiversity and is considered one of the best faunal and floral areas in Bihar.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Gautam Buddha Wildlife Sanctuary was formally constituted in 1976. Established on the lines of the Project Tiger, the sanctuary lies in the South-East part of Gaya district. It is highly recognized for its immense biodiversity and is widely regarded as one of the best areas for flora and fauna in the state of Bihar.
