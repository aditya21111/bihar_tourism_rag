OVERVIEW:
Gurunanak Shital Kund in Rajgir is an important Sikh pilgrimage destination woven around the tales of Guru Nanak's arrival in the town. Surrounded by several hot sulphurous springs, this particular kund is famous for its cold and pure water, a miracle attributed to Guru Nanak. Despite Rajgir having a minimal Sikh population, the site and its up-and-coming Gurudwara attract a steady flow of worshippers and tourists.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located amidst the famous hot springs of Rajgir, the Guru Nanak Shital Kund stands out as a unique spring that flows with cold, pure water. The site includes a beautiful white Gurudwara structure. The area draws visitors who are captivated by the contrasting natural phenomenon of a cold spring nestled among 22 hot sulphurous springs, heavily linking the site to the serene teachings and miracles of Guru Nanak.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The kund holds immense cultural and religious significance for the Sikh community as it commemorates the visit of Guru Nanak during one of his pilgrimages. It represents the historical footprint of the Sikh faith in Bihar.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The tale of Sikh faith in Rajgir is woven around Guru Nanak's arrival. His first stop in the town was the Brahma Kund. It is said that the local residents earnestly requested him to do something about the prevalent hot sulphurous water. In response, he calmly pointed at one of the 22 hot springs, declaring that the water there would flow cold and pure. Ever since that miraculous event, the water at the Guru Nanak Shital Kund has flowed cold.
