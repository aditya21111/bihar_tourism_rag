OVERVIEW:
The Archaeological Museum in Nalanda houses an outstanding collection of artifacts, sculptures, and daily items unearthed during the extensive excavations of the ancient Nalanda University and its surrounding region. It is a vital attraction for anyone interested in the art, history, and daily life of the ancient Buddhist academic center.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The museum's exhibits are vast and well-preserved. It boasts an outstanding collection of undamaged statues of Lord Buddha. The sculptures on display are expertly carved on basalt stones, bronze, and other materials. Additionally, the museum showcases a variety of terracotta items, iron implements, stuccos, inscriptions on stones and copper, ancient coins, potteries, and unique items like stupas on leaf of pipal tree and charred rice samples.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
These artifacts provide a profound insight into the material culture, artistic excellence, and daily operations of Nalanda University and its residents spanning centuries.

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
The Archaeological Museum in Nalanda is a repository of outstanding artifacts excavated from the ancient Nalanda University. The collection includes undamaged Buddha statues, basalt and bronze sculptures, terracotta items, coins, copper inscriptions, and unique exhibits like charred rice and pipal leaf stupas. It is accessible via Patna Airport and Nalanda/Rajgir railway stations.
