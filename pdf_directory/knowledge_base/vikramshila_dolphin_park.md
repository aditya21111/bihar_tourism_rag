OVERVIEW:
The Vikramshila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary is a protected wildlife area located in the Bhagalpur District of Bihar. Stretching over 60 kilometers of the Ganges River from Sultanganj to Kahalgaon, it was notified in 1991 to conserve the endangered Gangetic dolphins. This sanctuary is a vital habitat, hosting about half of the remaining population of these dolphins, which have been declared the national aquatic animal of India.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The sanctuary encompasses a 60 kilometers stretch of the Ganges River flowing from Sultanganj to Kahalgaon in the Bhagalpur district. It is a critical protected area established to safeguard the endangered Gangetic dolphins in Asia. While these dolphins were once found in abundance, only a few hundred remain today, of which half are found in this sanctuary.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The sanctuary was officially notified in 1991 to protect the endangered Gangetic dolphins. The Gangetic Dolphin holds high significance as it has been declared the national aquatic animal of India.
