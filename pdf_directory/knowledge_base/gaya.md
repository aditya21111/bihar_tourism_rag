OVERVIEW:
Gaya is a world-renowned destination famous for its deep association with Lord Buddha. Beyond its immense religious significance, Gaya is also a prominent culinary hotspot, highly celebrated for its traditional local sweets: Tilkut and Anarsa. 

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
While Gaya attracts pilgrims and tourists globally due to its connection to Lord Buddha, its local food culture is equally enticing. The city is famous for Tilkut, a sweet made with white sesame seeds (til) and sugar, and Anarsa, which is prepared from a combination of rice flour, white sesame seeds, and sugar. Ramna Road in Gaya is highlighted as the primary hub where visitors should head to taste these authentic traditional sweets.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Gaya holds monumental historical and religious importance through its association with Lord Buddha. Furthermore, its traditional sweets like Tilkut and Anarsa form an integral part of its local cultural heritage.
