OVERVIEW:
The Ashokan Pillar at Kolhua is a prominent ancient monument located at a famous Buddhist excavation site in Vaishali. Situated approximately 65 km North-West of Patna, the site features a majestic pillar erected by Emperor Ashoka, showcasing a grand statue of a lion on its top. Extensive excavations have also revealed numerous other artifacts related to Buddhism.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Kolhua is a widely recognized Buddhist excavation site. It is located roughly 65 km North-West of Patna. The central attraction of the site is the famous Ashokan Pillar. At the top of this monolithic pillar is a beautifully carved statue of a lion. Surrounding excavations have uncovered many other historical articles and structures related to Buddhism.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
As an Ashokan Pillar, it serves as a testament to Emperor Ashoka's dedication to spreading Buddhism. It represents a major archaeological find that highlights the region's importance in early Buddhist history.
