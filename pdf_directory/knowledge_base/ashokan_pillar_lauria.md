OVERVIEW:
The Ashokan Pillar in Lauria Nandangarh, West Champaran, is a significant archaeological and historical site situated near the banks of the Burhi Gandak River. The village draws its name from the pillar ('laur') of Ashoka and the nearby Nandangarh stupa mound. The site features an ancient Ashokan pillar standing tall alongside 15 ancient stupa mounds dating back to the 3rd century BCE, making it an important destination for exploring India's ancient history.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Lauria Nandangarh is renowned for its historic Ashokan Pillar and the impressive Nandangarh stupa. Situated near the banks of the Burhi Gandak River, the site features a well-preserved pillar erected by Emperor Ashoka. The nearby Nandangarh stupa dates back to the 3rd century BCE. It is characterized by 15 stupa mounds arranged in three distinct rows. Each row extends upwards of 600 meters. The first row begins near the Ashokan pillar and extends from East to West, while the other two rows are positioned at right angles to it, running parallel to each other.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The site is of immense historical and archaeological importance due to the presence of the Ashokan Pillar and the ancient stupa mounds. The Nandangarh stupa, dating back to the 3rd century BCE, reflects the architectural and religious landscape of ancient India during the Mauryan period. The village's name itself is derived from the 'laur' (pillar) and the stupa mound, signifying the lasting legacy of Emperor Ashoka's reign in the region.

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
The Ashokan Pillar in Lauria Nandangarh, West Champaran, is an important historical site featuring a monumental pillar erected by Emperor Ashoka and the 3rd-century BCE Nandangarh Stupa. Situated near the Burhi Gandak River, the site includes 15 stupa mounds arranged in three rows. Visitors can reach the site via Patna Airport (235 kms) or Bettiah Railway Station (25 kms). Open from 09:00 AM to 04:00 PM between October and March, the site offers parking, drinking water, and allows photography. Nearby attractions include the Valmiki Tiger Reserve and Amavaman.
