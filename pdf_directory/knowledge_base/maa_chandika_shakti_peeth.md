OVERVIEW:
Maa Chandika Shakti Peeth, also known as Chandika Sthan, is a sacred Hindu temple situated in Munger. It is renowned as one of the fifty-one Shakti Peethas—places of worship consecrated to the goddess Shakti. Located on the Northeast corner of Munger, just two kilometers from the town, it is considered a Siddhi-Peetha and is one of the most sanctified temples in the region.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Chandika Sthan is a highly venerated Hindu temple and a Siddhi-Peetha. It draws a large number of devotees seeking spiritual blessings. The temple represents a crucial center of worship for followers of the Divine Mother Chandi and plays a major role in the local religious landscape.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
As one of the fifty-one Shakti Peethas, the temple holds supreme religious importance in Hinduism. Being a Siddhi-Peetha, it is considered one of the most sacred and sanctified temples, deeply embedded in Hindu folklore and spirituality.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to legendary tales and Hindu folklore, Lord Shiva took the corpse of Sati and wandered in anger to save the world. It is believed that the left eye of Sati fell at Munger, which subsequently developed into this place of worship dedicated to the Divine Mother Chandi.
