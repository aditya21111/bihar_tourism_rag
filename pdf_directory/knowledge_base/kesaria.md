OVERVIEW:
Kesaria in East Champaran is home to a lofty brick mound capped by a large solid brick tower. It is considered to be the highest of the Buddhist Stupas. The mound is an ancient ruin and a significant archaeological and religious site believed to have been built between 200-700 AD.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The site features a massive loft brick mound which is capped by a large solid brick tower. The mound is currently a ruin, measuring 68 feet in diameter at its base and reaching a total height of 104 feet. It is widely considered to be the highest of the Buddhist Stupas.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It is an important Buddhist historical and archaeological site, believed to have been built some time between 200-700 AD. It is renowned as the highest Buddhist Stupa.
