OVERVIEW:
Jain Temple, Kundalpur is an essential religious complex comprising six Jain temples located in Kundalpur village near Nalanda, Bihar. It is revered as one of the most important Jain pilgrimages in Bihar. Historically, Kundalpur was an ancient capital city ruled by the Licchavi. The site is immensely significant as the believed birthplace of Mahavira, the 24th Tirthankara of Jainism, as well as the birthplace of four of his eleven Ganadhara.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Kundalpur temple complex features the ancient temple site and a new temple complex situated 1 kilometer away. The new complex consists of five beautiful temples constructed using stones from Jaisalmer. These temples house magnificent idols of Lord Mahavira, Rishabhanatha, and Gautama Swami. In total, the Kundalpur temple complex houses 72 idols of Tirthankaras installed in separate temples, creating a deeply spiritual environment for devotees and pilgrims.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Kundalpur holds immense historical and religious importance as an ancient capital ruled by the Licchavi. It is believed to be the birthplace of Lord Mahavira (the 24th Tirthankara) and four of his eleven Ganadhara, including Gautama Swami. The location is an unmissable pilgrimage destination for followers of Jainism.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The site is steeped in the tradition that it is the precise birthplace of Lord Mahavira and four of his chief disciples (Ganadhara), elevating its spiritual significance for Jains worldwide.
