OVERVIEW:
Lachhuar is a significant Jain pilgrimage site situated in the Jamui district, in a valley between two parallel ranges of hills. It is historically and religiously important as it is believed to be the birthplace of Lord Mahavir Swami. The site features numerous Jain temples, ancient statues of Mahavira, and dharamshalas built for pilgrims.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Lachhuar is situated about 8 km west of Simaria and 7 km south of Sikandra. It is nestled in a valley between two parallel ranges of hills. The site houses small statues of Mahavira dating back to Samvat 1505. Additionally, the location boasts a large number of Jain temples and dharamshalas, which were built in 1874 by Rai Dhanpat Singh Bahadur of Murshidabad for the benefit of visiting Jain pilgrims.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It holds tremendous significance for Jains as it is believed to be the birthplace of Lord Mahavir Swami. The presence of ancient statues dating back to Samvat 1505 highlights its deep historical roots.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
