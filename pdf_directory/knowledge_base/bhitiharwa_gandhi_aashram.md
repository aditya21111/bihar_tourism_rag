OVERVIEW:
Bhitiharwa Gandhi Aashram is a historic settlement located in the Gaunaha block of West Champaran. It holds great significance in Indian history as the hamlet where Mahatma Gandhi initiated his liberation campaign, famously known as the 'Champaran Satyagraha'. The settlement houses an Ashram that serves as an important Gandhian pilgrimage site, drawing visitors interested in the roots of India's freedom struggle.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Bhitiharwa Gandhi Aashram is a small but historically monumental hamlet located in the Gaunaha block of West Champaran. It is the very place from which Mahatma Gandhi launched his pioneering liberation campaign. The settlement still contains the Ashram that was central to his activities during that era. Today, the Ashram stands as a revered site for Gandhian pilgrimage, preserving the memories and the legacy of the historic Champaran Satyagraha.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The site is of paramount historical importance as the birthplace of the 'Champaran Satyagraha', marking Mahatma Gandhi's first major liberation campaign in Indian history. The Ashram stands as a testament to the early struggles of the Indian independence movement and serves as a pilgrimage site for followers of Gandhian philosophy and history enthusiasts.

It is the hamlet where Mahatma Gandhi launched his first liberation campaign, the famous 'Champaran Satyagraha'.
