OVERVIEW:
Maa Ugratara Mandir is an ancient and prominent temple situated in Mahishi village, approximately 17 Kms west of Saharsa station. Housing a very old idol of Bhagwati Tara, the temple draws devotees from far and wide. The main deity is flanked by two smaller female deities, worshipped by the people as Ekjata and Nil Saraswati, making it a highly significant spiritual destination.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located in the historical Mahishi village, the temple centers around the main idol of Bhagwati Tara, which is reputed to be very ancient. Flanking the main deity on either side are two smaller female deities. These deities are deeply revered by locals and visiting devotees as Ekjata and Nil Saraswati. The temple's ancient architecture and deeply rooted traditions make it a major spiritual landmark in the region.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The temple is considered highly ancient and holds tremendous religious and cultural importance for devotees who travel from far and wide to worship Bhagwati Tara, Ekjata, and Nil Saraswati.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The traditional worship includes not only the main deity, Bhagwati Tara, but also the two smaller flanking female deities, who are believed to be Ekjata and Nil Saraswati.
