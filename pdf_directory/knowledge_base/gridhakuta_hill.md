OVERVIEW:
Gridhakuta Hill, widely known as the Vulture Peak or Holy Eagle Peak, is a highly sacred Buddhist site located in Rajgir. Recognized as the Buddha's favorite retreat, it served as the scene for many of his profound discourses. The hill gets its distinctive name due to its striking resemblance to a sitting vulture with folded wings. It is a major pilgrimage destination and a revered place for spiritual training and retreat.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Gridhakuta Hill, or Vulture Peak, is a prominent mountain located in Rajgir. It is characterized by its unique rock formations which remarkably resemble a sitting vulture with its wings folded, hence giving the hill its name. The site holds a peaceful and spiritual environment. According to tradition, this mountain was one of several sites frequently visited by the Buddha and his community of disciples for meditation, training, and retreat. The serene summit continues to attract pilgrims from around the world.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The hill holds immense religious and historical significance in Buddhism. It is historically recognized as the Buddha's favorite retreat in Rajgir and the very location where he delivered many of his most important discourses.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to tradition, Vulture Peak Mountain was a frequently used site by the Buddha and his disciples for both training and retreat.
