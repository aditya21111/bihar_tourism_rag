OVERVIEW:
Tutla Bhawani, also known as Tutrahi, is a spectacular natural waterfall and religious site located in the scenic valley near Tilauthu, Rohtas district. It offers a breathtaking view with the Kachuar river flowing through a lush, mile-long valley nestled between high hills. At the site, a beautiful platform inside the waterfall houses the statue of Maa Jagaddhatri Mahishmardini Durga. Recently developed as an eco-tourism destination, it provides wonderful amenities for tourists to enjoy nature and spirituality.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Tutla Bhawani is situated approximately 20 Kms south-west from Dehri-on-Sone and about 8 Kms south-west of Tilauthu. Considered the most picturesque place in Rohtas district, the valley stretches for about 1 mile, deeply enveloped in greenery between two high hills on the north-west and south-east sides. The beautiful Kachuar river flows right in front of the waterfall, creating a mesmerizing landscape. Inside the waterfall on its right (south) side, positioned at a slight height, is a platform where the statue of Maa Jagaddhatri Mahishmardini Durga resides. The Forest Department has recently transformed this site into an eco-tourism destination, adding modern facilities like a swing pool, e-rickshaws, and road access.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The site is highly revered as a Shaktipith, housing the sacred statue of Maa Jagaddhatri Mahishmardini Durga, drawing religious devotees alongside nature lovers.
