OVERVIEW:
Ratnaghar, located in Bodhgaya, is an important meditative site where Lord Buddha spent his fourth week following his enlightenment. It is revered for a miraculous spiritual event involving a divine emanation of light and is marked today by a statue of the Buddha.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Ratnaghar commemorates a profound phase of Buddha's post-enlightenment meditation. During his meditation here, a holy light radiating in five different colors came out from his persona. Today, visitors can see a beautiful statue of the Buddha placed at this very site.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The five colors that emanated from Buddha at this location—representing purity, wisdom, and compassion—were later adopted as the colors present in the modern international Buddhist flag, linking the history of Ratnaghar to global Buddhist identity.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to traditions, Buddha spent the 4th week after enlightenment meditating here. It was during this deep meditation that his body miraculously emitted rays of light in five different colors.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
* Site where Buddha spent the 4th week after enlightenment meditating.
* The place where the five colors of the Buddhist flag originated from his aura.
* Houses a statue of the Buddha.
