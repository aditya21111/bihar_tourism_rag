OVERVIEW:
Katyayani Devi Sthan is a revered religious site situated near the Dhamahra Rail Station in Khagaria district. The site is associated with Rishi Katyayan and the incarnation of Ma Durga in child form. The temple features an ornate multi-tiered colorful structure reflecting traditional religious architecture.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The temple is built on the bank of the river Kaushiki (now Koshi). It is an elaborate temple featuring a tall main spire surrounded by smaller domes, brightly painted and adorned with traditional architectural elements. The shrine is dedicated to Goddess Katyayani, who is believed to be the child avatar of Ma Durga.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The site holds great religious and mythological significance as it is believed to be the place where Rishi Katyayan performed his Tapasya (penance) and where Ma Durga took the avatar of his daughter.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to legend, Rishi Katyayan was performing Tapasya on the bank of the river Kaushiki (now known as Koshi). It is believed that Ma Durga, the deity of Shakti, took 'avtar' (incarnation) in a child form here and was accepted by the Rishi as his daughter. Hence, she is worshipped here as Katyayani.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
* Mythological site of Rishi Katyayan's Tapasya.
* Believed to be the birthplace of Ma Durga's child avatar, Katyayani.
* Located on the banks of the historical Kaushiki (Koshi) river.
