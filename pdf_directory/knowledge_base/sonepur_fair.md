OVERVIEW:
Sonepur Fair is Asia's largest cattle fair and a vibrant cultural extravaganza. Taking place around the confluence of the Ganges and Gandak rivers, it attracts pilgrims, traders, and tourists from all over. Besides trading livestock, the fair features cultural performances, religious rituals, and a diverse array of handicrafts, making it a captivating and unique event in the cultural calendar of Bihar.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
An enormous and vibrant annual fair held at the confluence of the Ganges and Gandak rivers. Primarily known as Asia's largest cattle and livestock market, the fair transforms the area into a massive cultural hub. It features large-scale livestock trading, alongside cultural performances, the sale of local handicrafts, and the observance of various religious rituals.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It holds immense cultural importance in Bihar's calendar. As Asia's largest cattle fair, it brings together long-standing agrarian trading traditions with deep-rooted religious rituals and regional cultural arts.
