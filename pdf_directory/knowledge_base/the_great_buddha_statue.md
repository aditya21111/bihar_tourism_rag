OVERVIEW:
The Great Buddha statue (Daibutsu) is one of the popular stops on the Buddhist pilgrimage and tourist routes in Bodh Gaya, Bihar. The statue is 19.5 m (64 ft) high representing the Buddha seated in a meditation pose, or dhyana mudra, on a lotus in the open air. Located in a garden at the end of Temple Street, it is surrounded by smaller sculptures of Buddha's ten principal disciples, five on each side.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Great Buddha Statue, also known as Daibutsu, is an impressive 19.5 meters (64 feet) tall outdoor statue. It portrays Gautama Buddha seated in a meditation pose (dhyana mudra) on a large lotus. The statue is set within a garden at the end of Temple Street and features smaller sculptures of the ten principal disciples of Buddha flanking it, five on either side.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It is a major monument on the Buddhist pilgrimage route in Bodh Gaya, representing the serenity and meditation of Buddha.
