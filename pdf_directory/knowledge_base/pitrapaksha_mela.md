OVERVIEW:
Gaya is world-famous for a yearly occasion called 'Pitra Paksha Mela'. During this profound event, individuals from all over the world come here to pay homage to their ancestors (Pitras) through food offerings. Rituals like Shraddh, Tarpan, and Pind Daan are performed to please the departed souls and help them achieve liberation from the cycle of birth, life, and death.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
A massive annual religious gathering held in Gaya dedicated to ancestor worship. The Mela sees thousands of devotees gathering to perform sacred Hindu rites such as Shraddh, Tarpan, and Pind Daan. These rituals involve food offerings meant to bring peace to the souls of ancestors (Pitras). 

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Gaya holds immense religious significance in Hinduism as the premier destination for ancestor worship and Pind Daan. The performance of these rituals is believed to release departed souls from the infinite cycle of birth, life, and death.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The tradition revolves around paying homage to ancestors (Pitras) and conducting specific rituals to free them from the cycle of rebirth.
