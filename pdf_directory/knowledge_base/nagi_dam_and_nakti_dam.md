OVERVIEW:
Nagi Dam and Nakti Dam are popular, closely situated destinations in Jamui. Known for their scenic beauty amid picturesque hills and dense forests, they are also recognized as bird sanctuaries hosting migratory birds from Eurasia and Central Asia.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Nagi Dam and Nakti Dam are situated near each other in the Jamui district and are renowned for their scenic beauty, surrounded by picturesque hills and dense forests. The water from the dams serves as a crucial irrigation source for local farmers. The area is officially recognized as the Nagi-Nakti Bird Sanctuaries, which serve as home to a wide variety of indigenous bird species and migratory birds that arrive during winters from regions like Eurasia, Central Asia, the Arctic Circle, Russia, and Northern China.
