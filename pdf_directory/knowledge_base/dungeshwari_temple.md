OVERVIEW:
The caves in Dungeshwari Hills are said to have protected Lord Buddha for six years before he descended to Bodhgaya to attain enlightenment. These historic caves, located about 12-15 Kms from Gaya along the Falgu river, now house several temples visited by pilgrims following Lord Buddha's journey. Although the caves may lack striking visual appeal, they are reputed to offer a surreal experience due to their transcendental energy. One of the cave temples is dedicated to the Hindu goddess, Dungeshwari, adding to the site's rich religious tapestry.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located in the Dungeshwari Hills along the Falgu River, about 12-15 Kms from Gaya, the site consists of caves that housed Lord Buddha for six years prior to his enlightenment. Today, the caves house several temples. While the physical caves may be visually plain, they hold immense spiritual value. One of the cave temples is dedicated specifically to the Hindu Goddess Dungeshwari, making the site significant for both Buddhist and Hindu pilgrims. 

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
These caves hold immense historical and religious significance as they sheltered Lord Buddha for six years before his enlightenment. The site was also mentioned by the famous Chinese pilgrim Hiuen Tsang in his travelogue. It serves as a major pilgrimage site for those following the trail of Buddha's journey.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
It is believed that these very caves protected Lord Buddha for six years before his descent to Bodhgaya. The site is imbued with transcendental energy, and one of the cave temples serves as a shrine to the Hindu goddess Dungeshwari.
