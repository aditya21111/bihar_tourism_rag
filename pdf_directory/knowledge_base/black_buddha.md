OVERVIEW:
Near the ruins of Nalanda University is a modern temple named the Black Buddha temple, also known locally as Telia Bhairav. It features an ancient large black Buddha image in the Bhumisparsha Mudra, linking ancient heritage with modern worship practices.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The temple has emerged near Temple 14 of the Nalanda University ruins. It houses an ancient large black Buddha image in the Bhumisparsha Mudra. The locals refer to it as Telia Bhairav, where "tel" refers to the use of oil as a protective coating on the statue. Replicas of this Black Buddha image have been installed in temples across Thailand, demonstrating its international religious significance.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The idol is an ancient Buddha image that has become a center of modern worship. Its replicas being installed in Thailand highlight its importance in global Buddhism and cross-cultural religious exchange.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The local name "Telia Bhairav" derives from the tradition of applying oil ("tel") to the statue as a protective coating, reflecting the local customs and devotion.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
* Ancient large black Buddha image in Bhumisparsha Mudra.
* Known locally as Telia Bhairav due to oil coating traditions.
* Replicas of the image are installed in Thailand temples.
