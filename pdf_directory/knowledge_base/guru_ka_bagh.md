OVERVIEW:
Guru ka Bagh is a historic religious shrine in Patna, commemorating the historic meeting of Guru Tegh Bahadur and the young Guru Gobind Singh after a four-year odyssey.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located about three kilometers east of Takhat Sri Harimandir Sahib, Guru ka Bagh was originally a garden belonging to the nobles of Patna, Nawabs Rahim Bakhsh and Karim Bakhsh. This is the sacred spot where Guru Tegh Bahadur first alighted and was received by the Patna sangat along with young Guru Gobind Singh after his four-year-long odyssey. A shrine was established here to commemorate this first meeting. The present building was constructed during the 1970s and 1980s. Visitors can still see an old well that is still in use and the dried stump of the Imli tree under which the meeting took place.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It is highly significant in Sikh history as the location of the reunion between Guru Tegh Bahadur and his son, Guru Gobind Singh, along with the local sangat.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The site marks the historical reunion where the sangat of Patna and young Guru Gobind Singh received Guru Tegh Bahadur under an Imli tree.
