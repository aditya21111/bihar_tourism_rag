OVERVIEW:
Vishwa Shanti Stupa in Rajgir is a magnificent white marble monument situated on Ratnagiri Hill. Built in 1969, the 129 ft tall stupa radiates immense spiritual and historical importance, offering panoramic views of the ancient city of Rajgir. The gentle sounds of the gong here awaken one's spirituality.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This white marble stupa rises 129 ft high on the scenic Ratnagiri Hill. Its circular dome is adorned with four golden statues of the Buddha. Reaching the stupa is an experience in itself; it can be accessed by a stairway, a traditional chairlift car, or a newly built cabin car ropeway. 

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Rajgir, the ancient city once named Vasumati by King Vasu, was the capital of Bimbisara and a prominent center for Hindus, Buddhists, and Jains. Lord Buddha and Lord Mahavir spent many years in this region. The Vishwa Shanti Stupa was built to promote global peace, resting in an area steeped in profound historical and archaeological importance.
