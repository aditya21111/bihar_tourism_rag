Wildlife Area

OVERVIEW:
Rajgir Nature & Zoo Safari is a unique 500-hectare destination located in the Rajgir Forest. It features the first Glass Sky Walk of the country and offers five distinct safari zones to witness diverse flora and fauna, making it a major attraction for national and international tourists.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Nature Safari is spread over an area of around 500 hectares in the Rajgir-Jethian Path, nestled between the Vaibhavgiri and Swarnagiri mountains in the forest area. It hosts the first Glass Sky Walk of the country. The Zoo Safari section features five zones: Herbivore Safari, Bear Safari, Leopard Safari, Tiger Safari, and Lion Safari, which together accommodate over 250 herbivores and carnivores. It provides visitors an opportunity to explore the wilderness and witness diverse regional flora and fauna.

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
Rajgir Nature & Zoo Safari is a 500-hectare wildlife destination in Nalanda, Bihar, situated between the Vaibhavgiri and Swarnagiri mountains. It features India's first Glass Sky Walk and offers five safari zones (Herbivore, Bear, Leopard, Tiger, Lion) with over 250 animals. Accessible via Patna Airport (110 KM) and Rajgir Railway Station.
