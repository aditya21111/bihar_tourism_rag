OVERVIEW:
Udaipur Wildlife Sanctuary is a protected area located in the West Champaran district. Spread over approximately 8.74 square kilometres, the sanctuary is predominantly a forest wetland situated on an oxbow lake sareyaman. It is well-known for its diverse flora, fauna, and rich avifauna.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Spread over an area of approximately 8.74 square kilometres, the Udaipur Wildlife Sanctuary is a predominantly forest wetland located on an oxbow lake known as sareyaman. It is one of the important sites for migratory birds in Bihar and hosts a rich avifauna consisting of a variety of resident and migratory water birds.
