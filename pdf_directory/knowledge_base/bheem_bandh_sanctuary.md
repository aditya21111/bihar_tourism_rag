OVERVIEW:
Bheem Bandh Sanctuary is a picturesque natural retreat situated south of the Ganges River, at the northern edge of the Chota Nagpur Plateau and west of Santhal Pargana in Munger. Known for its valleys, foothills, and exceptional hot springs, the sanctuary is an area of profound natural beauty. The Bhimbandh spring holds the highest temperature and discharge, establishing it as an area with high geothermal energy potential. 

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located in a scenic valley at the foothills, the sanctuary is home to several natural hot springs, the finest of which include Bhimbandh, Sita Kund, and Rishi Kund. These hot springs impressively maintain nearly the same temperature year-round. The Bhimbandh spring records the hottest temperatures ranging between 52°C to 65°C, boasting significant geothermal energy potential. The sanctuary serves as a major tourist interest, enriched by numerous natural spots nearby like waterfalls and lakes.
