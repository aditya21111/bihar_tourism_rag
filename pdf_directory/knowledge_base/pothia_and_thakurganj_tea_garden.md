OVERVIEW:
While tea gardens are generally associated with Assam and Darjeeling, Bihar holds its own flourishing tea gardens developed in Pothia and Thakurganj, Kishanganj. Supported by favorable soil quality and a natural drainage system, these tea estates present beautiful, lush green landscapes. The Barman Tea Garden in Thakurganj is highlighted as a top player among tea manufacturers in Kishanganj.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The attraction comprises expansive, scenic tea plantations spread across Pothia and Thakurganj. Favorable agricultural conditions such as high soil quality and a natural drainage system have allowed tea cultivation to thrive here. Visitors can experience vast stretches of green tea bushes where workers can be seen harvesting tea leaves. Barman Tea Garden is one of the most prominent tea manufacturers operating in this scenic region.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Represents the unique agricultural and economic expansion of tea cultivation in Bihar, challenging the traditional view that tea is only grown in neighboring eastern states.
