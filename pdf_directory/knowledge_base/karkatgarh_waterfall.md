Karkatgarh Waterfall is a historically significant natural site in Kaimur known for its lush greenery and serene environment. It historically served as a crocodile hunting place for Mughal rulers and British officials.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Karkatgarh Waterfall boasts a rich history rooted in the Mughal and British eras. Historically, this place was used as a crocodile hunting ground by Mughal rulers and British officials. Today, the location's lush greenery and stunningly serene environment make it a natural crowd puller and an attractive destination for tourists.
