OVERVIEW:
Mangla Gauri temple is a revered Shakti Peetham located in Gaya, Bihar. It is counted among the eighteen maha shaktipeeths and is dedicated to Sati or the Mother Goddess. Situated on top of the Mangalagauri hill in the predominantly Vaishnavite pilgrimage center of Gaya, the Goddess is worshiped here as the Goddess of benevolence. The temple holds immense religious importance and is mentioned in several ancient scriptures.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The present temple structure dates back to the 15th century. It faces east and is built on top of the Mangalagauri hill, accessible via a flight of steps and a motorable road. The sanctum of the temple houses the symbol of the Goddess and features some finely carved ancient relief sculptures. Additionally, the temple complex includes two minor shrines dedicated to Lord Shiva and houses images of Mahishasura Mardini, Durga, and Dakshina Kali.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The temple is a highly significant shrine for Hindus, constituting an Upa-Shakti Peeth. It has been mentioned in sacred texts including the Padma Purana, Vayu Purana, Agni Purana, Devi Bhagvata Purana, Markandeya Purana, as well as other scriptures and tantric works. The temple is considered one of the eighteen maha shaktipeeths.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to Hindu mythology, the temple constitutes an Upa-Shakti Peeth where it is believed that a part of the body of Sati fell. Mangalagauri is worshipped here as the Goddess of benevolence.

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
Mangla Gauri Mandir is an ancient 15th-century Shakti Peetham located on Mangalagauri hill in Gaya, Bihar. Dedicated to Goddess Sati, it is one of the eighteen maha shaktipeeths and is mentioned in scriptures like the Padma Purana and Vayu Purana. The temple features ancient relief sculptures and shrines to Shiva, Durga, and Kali. It is open from 05:00 AM to 12:00 PM and is best visited from October to March. The site offers parking, souvenir shops, and is 9 km from Gaya International Airport and 5 km from Gaya Junction. Nearby attractions include Vishnupad Mandir (1 km) and Mahabodhi Mandir (17 km).
