OVERVIEW:
The 80 ft. Buddha Stupa, also referred to as the Giant Buddha statue, is a prominent stop on the Buddhist pilgrimage and tourist routes in Bodhgaya. It features a massive statue of Lord Buddha seated on a lotus in the open air, measuring an impressive 80 ft in total architectural height.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This architectural marvel consists of a Giant Buddha statue situated in the open air. The statue depicts Lord Buddha in a meditation pose, also known as the dhyana mudra, seated gracefully on a lotus. While the statue itself measures 19.507 meters (64.00 feet) in height, the total height of the architecture reaches 80 feet, making it a monumental landmark for visitors.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It serves as a major landmark for Buddhist pilgrimage and tourism in Bodhgaya, drawing visitors looking to experience the serene meditation pose of Lord Buddha.
