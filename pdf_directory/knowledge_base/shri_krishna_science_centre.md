OVERVIEW:
Shri Krishna Science Centre is a prominent educational and scientific attraction located near Gandhi Maidan in Patna. Established in 1978, it holds the distinction of being the first regional-level science center in the entire state of Bihar. Named after the first Chief Minister of Bihar, Shri Krishna Singh, the center serves as a hub for interactive science exhibits and learning, making it an excellent destination for students and curious minds.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located close to the iconic Gandhi Maidan in Patna, the Shri Krishna Science Centre was established in the year 1978. It was pioneered as the first regional level science center in Bihar. The institution was named in honor of Shri Krishna Singh, who was the first Chief Minister of Bihar. The center features a vibrant exterior with science-themed murals, gears, and models, signifying its focus on scientific education and public engagement.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
