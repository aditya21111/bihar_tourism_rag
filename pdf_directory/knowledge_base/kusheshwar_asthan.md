OVERVIEW:
Kusheshwar Asthan, also known as Baba Kusheshwar Nath and Baba Dham of Mithila, is an important Hindu pilgrimage site located in Darbhanga, Bihar. Situated at the confluence of three rivers, this temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva and sees many devotees daily offering prayers. The site is notably famous for the large mela organized during the holy month of Shrawan. Beyond its religious significance, the area is also a haven for bird lovers as it features a lake where a large number of rare species of birds migrate during the winter season.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The temple complex of Kusheshwar Asthan is a prominent spiritual destination built at the sacred confluence of three rivers. Inside the complex lies a sacred well known as Chandrakup, which instills a deep sense of peace in the devotees who visit. It is deeply believed that Lord Shiva fulfills the wishes of everyone who comes here. Additionally, the location boasts ecological importance, hosting a lake that becomes a vibrant habitat for rare migratory birds during the winters, making it an attractive spot for nature and bird lovers.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It holds major cultural importance as the "Baba Dham of Mithila," serving as a central hub for Lord Shiva devotees in the region. The grand mela during Shrawan highlights its massive religious following.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
There is a strong belief that Lord Shiva fulfills the wishes of all devotees who pray at this temple. Devotees also hold deep reverence for the sacred well, Chandrakup, located within the temple complex, which is believed to bring immense peace to those who enter the temple.
