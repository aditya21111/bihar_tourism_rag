OVERVIEW:
Girihinda mountain is the identity of Sheikhpura district, standing about 800 feet high. The Shiva Parvati Temple, also known as Baba Kameshwar Nath Temple, is situated on top of this mountain. According to belief, this temple dates back to the Mahabharata period, established by Gadadhari Bhima and built overnight by Vishwakarma Ji under Lord Shiva's order. It is a prominent pilgrimage and historical spot in the region.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The mountain rises to an elevation of approximately 800 feet. At its peak lies the Shiva Parvati Temple, widely known as Baba Kameshwar Nath Temple. The temple houses a Shivling which is central to the area's religious life and is linked to the legendary figures of the Mahabharata.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The temple holds immense historical and mythological significance as it is believed to have been constructed during the Mahabharata period. It serves as a major religious and cultural identity for the Sheikhpura district.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to local folklore and belief, it was Gadadhari Bhima who established the Shivling on the mountain of Girihinda. Following this, upon the order of Lord Shiva, Vishwakarma Ji built the temple overnight, which later became known as Shiva Parvati Temple or Baba Kameshwar Nath Temple.
