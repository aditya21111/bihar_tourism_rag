OVERVIEW:
Someshwer Shivmandir is an age-old and highly revered temple located in the holy city of Areraj, East Champaran, Bihar. Serving as a prime pilgrimage destination in North Bihar, it attracts lakhs of pilgrims from various districts and neighboring Nepal, especially during the annual Shrawani Mela. The site is further enriched by the presence of a historical Ashokan pillar that attracts tourists throughout the year.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located 28 Km south-west of Motihari, the Someshwar Nath Mahadev Temple is a sacred historical shrine. The area is a hub of spiritual energy, highlighted by massive gatherings of devotees during festivals. Additionally, the complex or vicinity includes an Ashokan pillar, adding an archaeological and historical dimension to the spiritual journey of tourists.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Areraj is known as a holy city in North Bihar. The Someshwar Nath Mahadev Temple is age-old, holding significant religious importance for Hindus. The presence of an Ashokan pillar also denotes ancient historical and cultural importance rooted in the Mauryan era.
