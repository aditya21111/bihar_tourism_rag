OVERVIEW:
The Vikramshila Gangetic Dolphin Sanctuary is a protected area located in Bhagalpur. Spread over a 60-kilometre stretch of the Ganga River from Sultanganj to Kahalgaon, the sanctuary is dedicated to the conservation of the endangered Gangetic Dolphin, the national aquatic animal of India.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Established in 1991, the sanctuary covers an approximately 60-kilometre stretch of the Ganga River from Sultanganj to Kahalgaon. It focuses exclusively on the protection and conservation of the endangered Gangetic Dolphin, which also holds the status of the national aquatic animal of India.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The sanctuary was established in 1991 and is named after the ancient Vikramshila University that was located nearby.
