OVERVIEW:
Rohtas Garh Fort is a monumental and sprawling historical fortress situated on the upper course of the river Son, taking about two hours from Sasaram to reach the foot of the hill. Considered one of the largest forts in the world, it is spread over a massive 42 Sq Kms. With 83 gates and a network of underground places and tunnels, it is a formidable ancient structure housing significant monuments like Aina Mahal, Jamia Masjid, Habsh Khan's mausoleum, Ganesh Temple, Rohtasan Temple, and Devi Temple.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Rohtasgarh Fort is situated majestically on the upper course of the river Son, accessible by a two-hour journey from Sasaram to the hill's base. The fortress is exceptionally grand, heralded as one of the largest forts in the world, spreading across 42 square kilometers. It features a staggering 83 gates and a complex network of underground places and tunnels. The entrance begins with a gate adorned with a cupola, the first of many well-guarded entrances. The main gate is renowned as the Hathiya Pol or the Elephant Gate, named after the prominent figures of elephants. Inside, the fort encloses numerous historic structures including the Aina Mahal, Jamia Masjid, Habsh Khan's mausoleum, Ganesh Temple, Rohtasan Temple, and Devi Temple.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The fort stands as a symbol of immense historical and architectural power. It is recognized globally for its sheer scale, housing important religious and administrative structures like temples, mosques, and palaces within its walls.
