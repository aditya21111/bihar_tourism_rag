OVERVIEW:
The Bari Patan Devi Temple in Patna is a prominent north-facing shrine situated towards the Ganges river. Known for its sacred black stone statues, it houses the idols of the goddesses Mahakali, Maha Lakshmi, Maha Saraswati, along with Bhairav. The first three goddesses are traditionally attired in sarees. The temple serves as a major religious and spiritual center in the city.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Bari Patan Devi Temple faces north towards the Ganges river. It features idols made entirely of black stone. The main deities include Mahakali, Maha Lakshmi, Maha Saraswati, and Bhairav. All the idols are placed on simhasans (thrones) that measure about 4 square in cross section and stand at a height of about 7 feet (2.1 m). The three goddesses are distinctively adorned in sarees.

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
The Bari Patan Devi Temple in Patna is a prominent religious site facing the Ganges river. It features striking black stone idols of Mahakali, Maha Lakshmi, Maha Saraswati, and Bhairav placed on tall 7-feet simhasans. The best time to visit is from October to March between 04:00 AM and 08:00 PM. The site offers amenities like parking, restaurants, drinking water, and souvenir shops. It is located 17 kms from Patna Airport and 9 kms from Patna Junction. Nearby attractions include Sonar Toli Gurudwara, Takhat Sri Hari Mandir, Jalan Museum, Kangan Ghat, and Sabhyata Dwar.
