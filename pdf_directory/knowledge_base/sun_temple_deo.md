OVERVIEW:
Sun Temple, Deo in Aurangabad district is one of the most notable temples and a major crowd-puller in Bihar. Dedicated to Surya, the sun god, it is a solar shrine primarily known for Chhath Puja. The temple is unique as it faces west, the direction of the setting sun, rather than the usual rising sun. It is considered one of the most sacred places for sun worship and Chhath Puja.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The temple's sanctum features beautiful idols of the presiding deities, namely Vishnu, Surya, and Avalokitesawara. The structure is a mixture of Nagri Architecture, Dravidian architecture, and Vesara architecture. A distinct dome shape is carved above the Dev Sun Temple, with a gold urn placed over the dome.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The temple holds immense religious importance, particularly for Chhath Puja. It is one of the most sacred places in Bihar for sun worshipping. The architectural style showcases a blend of Nagri, Dravidian, and Vesara architectures, reflecting a rich cultural heritage.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The temple uniquely faces west, towards the setting sun, instead of the rising sun, which is a rare tradition among sun temples.
