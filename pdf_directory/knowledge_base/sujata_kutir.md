OVERVIEW:
Sujata Kutir in Bodhgaya marks the significant place where Lord Buddha was offered kheer by a girl named Sujata after he had realized the extremes of asceticism. A large circular brick stupa was built here in memory of this divine incident, serving as an important part of the Buddhist pilgrimage.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The site features a stupa which is a large circular brick structure built in the memory of the divine incident of Sujata offering kheer to Lord Buddha. This stupa was an integral part of the original landscape at Bodhgaya. Over the centuries, it was reinforced and enlarged several times.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It holds immense religious significance in Buddhism as it marks the turning point in Lord Buddha's path to enlightenment, where he abandoned extreme asceticism after accepting food (kheer) from Sujata.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to tradition, this is the exact place where Lord Buddha, weak from extreme asceticism, was offered kheer (rice pudding) by a girl named Sujata. This event helped him realize that the middle path was the true way to enlightenment.
