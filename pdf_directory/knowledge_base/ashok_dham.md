ATTRACTION: Ashok Dham

ALTERNATIVE NAMES:\
Indradamneshwar Mahadev Mandir

LOCATION:\
Ashok Dham Rajauna Chouki in Lakhisarai district, Bihar.

DISTRICT / REGION:\
Lakhisarai

CATEGORY:
Religious Site

OVERVIEW:
Ashok Dham is a significant temple complex located in Lakhisarai district. It is anchored by the Indradamneshwar Mahadev Mandir in the center, which is dedicated to the primary deity Lord Shiva. The main shrine is surrounded by three additional temples dedicated to Goddess Parvati, Nandi (the mount of Shiva), and Goddess Durga.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Ashok Dham is a sprawling temple complex known for its spiritual atmosphere and majestic temple structures. The central structure is the Indradamneshwar Mahadev Mandir, which honors Lord Shiva. Complementing the central temple are three adjacent shrines dedicated to Goddess Parvati, Nandi (Shiva's mount), and Goddess Durga. 

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The original temple at this location holds deep historical roots, having been built in the 12th century by King Indradyumna.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The site is traditionally tied to King Indradyumna who originally built a temple here in the 12th century.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
* Indradamneshwar Mahadev Mandir (Lord Shiva) in the center
* Surrounding temples for Goddess Parvati, Nandi, and Goddess Durga
* Original 12th-century historical foundation by King Indradyumna
