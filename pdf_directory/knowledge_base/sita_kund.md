OVERVIEW:
Sita Kund is a small but invaluable heritage temple located in Gaya, situated on the opposite bank of the river Falgu from the famous Vishnupad temple. Associated deeply with the Ramayana period, Sita Kund is revered as the site where Sita Mata donated the body of her father-in-law, King Dashrath. The location continues to be a witness to historical customs that locals carry forward to this day.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located on the banks of the Falgu river directly across from the Vishnupad temple, Sita Kund is a small temple of immense cultural significance. It stands as an invaluable cultural heritage site that has witnessed numerous cultural developments in the past. Today, visitors and locals still perform customs and traditions rooted in its ancient history.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Sita Kund holds great cultural and religious significance as a prominent site connected to the epic Ramayana. It is a living heritage where traditional customs established in ancient times are still practiced by devotees today.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to Hindu beliefs, Sita Mata donated the body of her father-in-law, King Dashrath, at this very location. Another legend states that Sita Mata took a bath in this pool while going into a 14-year exile along with Lord Rama and Lakshmana, which gave the site its name, "Sita Kund".

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
Sita Kund in Gaya is a highly revered small temple located on the Falgu river bank, directly opposite the Vishnupad temple. Rooted in Ramayana lore, it is celebrated as the site where Sita Mata performed rites for King Dashrath and where she bathed during her exile. The site, easily accessible via Gaya Junction (6 Kms) and Gaya Airport (10.1 Kms), features parking, drinking water, and souvenir shops. It remains a vibrant cultural heritage site where ancient customs are still practiced by locals and pilgrims.
