OVERVIEW:
Kundalpur Jain Mandir is a beautiful temple built of Jaisalmer stone, situated near the ruins of ancient Nalanda University. It is a revered site believed to be the birthplace of Gautam Gandharva, the first disciple of Lord Mahavira.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located in Kundalpur, closely adjacent to the ancient ruins of Nalanda University, this stunning temple is constructed using beautiful Jaisalmer stone. The temple houses the sacred statues of Lord Mahavira, Adinath Swami, and Gautam Gandharva. Moreover, the site holds deep historical roots as it is believed to be the birthplace of Gautam Gandharva, Lord Mahavira's first disciple. An ancient Jain temple also exists on the premises.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The temple is an essential religious destination for Jains due to its association with Gautam Gandharva, the first disciple of Lord Mahavira, and houses important deities alongside an ancient temple structure.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The site is traditionally believed to be the birthplace of Gautam Gandharva, the first disciple of Lord Mahavira.
