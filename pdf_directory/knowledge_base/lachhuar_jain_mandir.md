OVERVIEW:
Lachhuar Jain Mandir is an ancient Jain temple located inside a large 65-room Dharamshala in the Sikandra Block of Jamui. It features an incredibly old and heavy black stone idol of Lord Mahavira.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Situated on the way to Kshatriya Kund Gram, Lachhuar Jain Mandir is housed within a massive and historic Dharamshala that comprises 65 rooms intended for Jain pilgrims. The highlight of the temple is its magnificent idol of Lord Mahavira, which is carved from black stone, weighs around 250 kg, and boasts an antiquity of over 2,600 years. The temple is located approximately 20 km west of the Jamui District Headquarter.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The temple is historically and religiously significant for its over 2,600-year-old black stone idol of Lord Mahavira and its role as a major resting place (Dharamshala) for Jain pilgrims traveling to Kshatriya Kund Gram.
