OVERVIEW:
The 80 ft Buddha Statue is a towering and iconic landmark in Bodhgaya. Commissioned and consecrated in 1989, it was the first great Buddha statue built in modern India, symbolizing peace, magnitude, and the global reverence for Buddhism.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This gigantic statue stands at an impressive height of 80 feet, making it a prominent feature of the Bodhgaya skyline. It represents the Buddha in deep meditation. Its construction and inauguration were a major international Buddhist event, marking a modern revival of large-scale Buddhist monumental art in India.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
As the first great Buddha statue built in modern India, it holds immense cultural significance. The consecration ceremony in 1989 was a globally significant event attended by His Holiness the Dalai Lama, underscoring the spiritual bond between Indian heritage and global Buddhism.
