DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Mithila Haat is an essential cultural marketplace situated in the Jhanjharpur block of Madhubani district. It acts as a dedicated venue to showcase the intricate and world-renowned Mithila (Madhubani) paintings. Beyond art, it also offers traditional local cuisine. The Haat is a vital cultural hub where visitors can interact with the heritage of the region, purchase authentic art directly from creators, and support local artists.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It serves as a critical platform for the preservation and promotion of Mithila paintings, a rich traditional art form indigenous to the region. It supports the livelihood of local artists and sustains the cultural heritage of Madhubani.
