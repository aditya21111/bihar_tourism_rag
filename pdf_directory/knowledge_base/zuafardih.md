OVERVIEW:
Zuafardih is an important archaeological site in the Nalanda district, famous for the discovery of an ancient stupa by the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI). Excavations here have brought to light numerous artifacts belonging to the Mauryan period, adding depth to the historical landscape of the region.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The primary attraction of Zuafardih is the ancient stupa discovered during ASI excavations. Along with the structural remains, the site has yielded many daily use and decorative artifacts. These include iron implements, utensils, pieces of glass, bangles, and more, all tracing their origins back to the ancient Mauryan era.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The discovery of Mauryan-period artifacts and the ancient stupa highlights the early settlement and continuous historical significance of the Nalanda region long before the famous university reached its zenith.
