OVERVIEW:
Khanquah Mujeebia is a prominent monastery located in Phulwari Sharif, Patna. It is associated with the great Sufi saint Hazrat Tajul Aarfeen Makhdoom Shah Mujeebullah Quadri, who was born and breathed his last here.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The monastery is named Khanquah Mujeebia in honor of its founder, Hazrat Tajul Aarfeen Makhdoom Shah Mujeebullah Quadri. His grave is located under a tomb within the garden of the monastery. It is a place of peace and spiritual significance.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Hazrat Makhdoom Peer Mujeebullah Quadri was an ancient and miraculous personality of the Quadria Sect (Silsila). The monastery serves as a significant spiritual center for followers of the Quadria sect.

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
Khanquah Mujeebia is a renowned religious site and monastery situated in Phulwari Sharif, Patna. Founded by Hazrat Tajul Aarfeen Makhdoom Shah Mujeebullah Quadri, a prominent figure of the Quadria Sect, the site features his tomb set amidst a serene garden. Open from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM (October to March), it offers basic facilities such as parking and drinking water, and is located 6 kms from Patna Airport and 7 kms from Patna Junction. Nearby attractions include Patna Zoo, Hanuman Mandir, and Bihar Museum.
