OVERVIEW:
The Kesaria Stupa in East Champaran is a colossal Buddhist stupa that stands as a magnificent testament to ancient architectural grandeur. Dating back to the 3rd century BCE, with its current structure dating to the Gupta Dynasty (between 200 AD and 750 AD), it is believed to have been originally built by Emperor Ashoka. Standing at a remarkable height of 104 feet, it is one of the tallest stupas in the world. Its intricate carvings and historical significance attract pilgrims and history enthusiasts alike.

Kesaria Stupa is a colossal and monumental Buddhist structure located in East Champaran. It stands at an impressive height of 104 feet, which distinguishes it as one of the tallest stupas globally. The massive stupa is a prominent landmark drawing numerous visitors interested in its historical and architectural grandeur.
