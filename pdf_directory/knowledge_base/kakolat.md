OVERVIEW:
Kakolat is a stunning waterfall in the Nawada district, cascading from a height of approximately 160 feet. Surrounded by lush green hills and dense forests, it is a highly popular picnic spot offering breathtaking views of natural beauty.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Kakolat is one of the most popular tourist destinations in the Nawada district. It features a spectacular waterfall that cascades down from a height of roughly 160 feet. The waterfall is enveloped by lush green hills and dense forests, making it an ideal retreat and a very popular picnic spot among tourists. Visitors can enjoy the breathtaking views and the refreshing environment of the natural cascade.
