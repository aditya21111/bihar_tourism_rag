OVERVIEW:
Litti Chokha is an iconic main-course dish that forms the heart of Bihari cuisine. Renowned for its rustic and traditional preparation, it has gained international acclaim, serving as a must-experience culinary highlight for any visitor exploring the cultural fabric of Bihar.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Litti is a traditional baked wheat flour cake that is salted and stuffed with a special filling. The filling is made of sattu (fried gram flour) mixed with an assortment of special spices. Visually, it shares a resemblance to the Rajasthani Bati but carries distinct differences in taste and preparation. Once baked, the Litti is generously served with ghee. It is traditionally accompanied by Chokha, a flavorful side dish typically made from brinjal bharta (roasted eggplant) or mashed potato. 

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Litti Chokha is deeply rooted in the agricultural and traditional lifestyle of Bihar. It represents the authentic taste of the region and has grown from a local staple to a dish with a prominent international presence, symbolizing Bihari cultural heritage globally.
