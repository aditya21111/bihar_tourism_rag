OVERVIEW:
The Mandar Hill Ropeway was constructed by the Tourism Department in the Banka district to facilitate easy access for tourists. It provides a 4-minute panoramic ride from the lowlands to the mountain peak, which previously took about 1 hour on foot. Tourists can enjoy pleasant views of the valleys while easily reaching historical, mythological, and religious sites located on Mandar hill.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The ropeway offers an effortless ascent to Mandar Hill, providing spectacular views of the panoramic valleys below. It drastically reduces the travel time to the peak from a 1-hour climb to just 4 minutes. At the hill, there are several religious and mythological points of interest including Sita Kund, Lord Narsingh Cave Temple, Goshala, and Yoni Kund.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Mandar Hill holds historical, mythological, and religious importance. Various sites like Sita Kund, Lord Narsingh Cave Temple, Goshala, and Yoni Kund are located here and are significant from a religious point of view.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
Mandar Hill has strong mythological and religious associations, housing revered sites like Sita Kund and Lord Narsingh Cave Temple.
