OVERVIEW:
Maa Tarachandi Mandir, located in Sasaram, Rohtas, is the oldest and one of the most sacred temples in the region. It is highly revered as one of the 51 Siddha Shakti Pithas in India. The ancient temple is dedicated to Goddess Durga Maa Tara Chandi and holds immense mythological and religious significance for Hindu devotees.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Maa Tarachandi Mandir is an ancient and prominent shrine in Sasaram. Known as Maa Tara chandi Shakti Peeth, it is believed to be the abode of the goddess Durga Maa Tara Chandi. The temple attracts numerous pilgrims who come to offer prayers and seek blessings from the goddess. The site is a major center for worship and spiritual activities in the Rohtas district.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The temple is considered one of the 51 Siddha Shakti Pithas in India, making it a highly sacred and culturally significant religious site. It has ancient origins, originally called Maa Sati, and is deeply rooted in Hindu mythology and Pauranic texts.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to Pauranic legends, the "right eye" (Netra) of the corpse of Sati had fallen at this exact location when it was chopped off by Lord Vishnu with his "Sudarshan Chakra". Because of this event, the site is worshipped as a revered Shakti Peeth.
