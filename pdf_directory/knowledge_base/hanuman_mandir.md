OVERVIEW:
Hanuman Mandir in Rajbanshi Nagar, Patna, is a revered temple that draws a heavy footfall of devotees, particularly on Tuesdays and Saturdays. What started as a small place on Bailey Road has grown into a major spiritual hub known for being the only temple with a south-facing deity.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located strategically on Bailey Road in Patna, this temple experiences large gatherings of devotees, especially during major festivals, Tuesdays, and Saturdays. The temple's significant advantage is its prominent location which accommodates large crowds. A unique architectural and spiritual feature of this temple is that it has a south-facing deity.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The temple holds immense religious significance for the devotees of Lord Hanuman. The presence of a south-facing deity adds to the spiritual uniqueness of this site.
