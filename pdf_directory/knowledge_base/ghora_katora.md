OVERVIEW:
Ghora Katora, meaning "Horse Bowl", is a picturesque natural lake located near the city of Rajgir in Nalanda, Bihar. The lake’s shape resembles a horse and is surrounded by mountains on three sides. It is a stunning eco-tourism spot featuring a magnificent 70 feet high Buddha statue in its center, attracting migratory birds and offering a peaceful boating experience.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Surrounded by scenic mountains on three sides, Ghora Katora is a uniquely shaped natural lake that resembles a horse. In the middle of the serene waters stands a majestic 70 feet high Buddha statue. During the winter, the lake becomes a haven for nature lovers as it attracts various migratory birds from Siberia and Central Asia. The destination is equipped with paddle boating facilities, a cafeteria, and guest rooms, making it a perfect retreat for visitors.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The 70-foot high Buddha statue in the middle of the lake adds profound religious and cultural significance, aligning with the Buddhist heritage of the Rajgir region.

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:

Famous for a 70-foot high Buddha statue in its center, it attracts migratory birds from Siberia and Central Asia in winter.
