OVERVIEW:
Lauria Nandangarh is a small historical town in the West Champaran district. It has been a witness to many important historical events, particularly from the Maurya period. The site is famous for an excavated Ashoka Pillar and the nearby ruins of a huge stupa at Nandangarh, making it a key attraction on the Buddhist Circuit.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The primary attraction of this town is a prominent Ashoka Pillar found during excavations, which stands as a testament to the glorious history of the Maurya period. At a distance of 2 km southwest from the Ashokan Pillar lies a mound known as Nandangarh. This mound contains the vast remains of a huge ancient stupa.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The Ashoka Pillar and the Nandangarh stupa highlight the region's rich Mauryan history and its association with Buddhism during Emperor Ashoka's reign.
