OVERVIEW:
Bhimbandh Wildlife Sanctuary is a vast natural reserve occupying an area of about 682 sq. km in the south-west of Munger District in Bihar. The sanctuary spans the famous Kharagpur range, covering dense forests and featuring hot water springs with temperatures ranging from 52°C to 65°C round the year. It boasts a very rich and varied fauna and is an excellent destination for nature lovers and wildlife enthusiasts.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located in the south-west of Munger District, the sanctuary occupies 682 sq. km of area. It is part of the famous Kharagpur range and covers dense forests. One of its unique features is the presence of hot water springs which maintain an average temperature of about 52°C to 65°C throughout the year. The sanctuary is rich in biodiversity and is home to a varied fauna. Animal species found here include Wild Boars, Sloth Bear, Sambar Deer, Chital, Four-horned Antelope, Nilgai, and more than 100 species of resident birds.
