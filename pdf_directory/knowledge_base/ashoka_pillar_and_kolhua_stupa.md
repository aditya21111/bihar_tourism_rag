ATTRACTION: Ashoka Pillar & Kolhua Stupa

ALTERNATIVE NAMES:
The Lion Pillar at Kolhua

LOCATION:
Kolhua, near Vaishali

DISTRICT / REGION:
Vaishali Region

CATEGORY:
Historical Site / Monument

OVERVIEW:
The Ashoka Pillar & Kolhua Stupa in Vaishali commemorate a monumental event in Buddhist history: Lord Buddha's last sermon. Erected by Emperor Ashoka in the third century BCE, the site features a highly polished red sandstone pillar with a majestic life-size lion figure perched on top, situated beside a historic brick stupa.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located at Kolhua, the site boasts an impressive, highly polished pillar made of red sandstone. The pillar is topped by a bell-shaped capital that supports a life-size lion figure. This Lion Pillar was erected by Emperor Ashoka to mark the sacred spot where Lord Buddha preached his last sermon. Placed alongside the pillar is an ancient brick stupa, adding to the archaeological and religious grandeur of the location.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Lord Buddha visited Vaishali numerous times and delivered his final sermon at Kolhua. To honor this sacred event, Emperor Ashoka erected the Lion Pillar in the 3rd century BCE. It stands as a testament to the spread of Buddhism and Mauryan architectural brilliance.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
It is believed to be the exact location of Buddha's last sermon before his journey to Mahaparinirvana. 

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
* Highly polished red sandstone pillar from the 3rd century BCE.
* Bell-shaped capital with a life-size lion figure on top.
* Ancient brick stupa situated beside the pillar.
* Marks the site of Buddha's last sermon.
