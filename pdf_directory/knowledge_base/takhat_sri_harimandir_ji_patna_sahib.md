OVERVIEW:
Takhat Sri Harimandir Ji Patna Sahib is a deeply sacred Sikh pilgrimage site located in Patna. It is the revered birthplace of the 10th Sikh Guru, Guru Gobind Singh Ji Maharaj.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This stunning white marble edifice is a major Takhat (seat of authority) in Sikhism, marking the sacred birthplace of the 10th Guru, Guru Gobind Singh Ji Maharaj. The reconstruction of this holy site was initially undertaken in 1839 by Maharaja Ranjit Singh Ji, the first king of the Sikh Empire. The present gorgeous architectural structure that visitors see today was reconstructed in 1954.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It is an incredibly holy site for Sikhs worldwide, being one of the Takhats and the precise birthplace of Guru Gobind Singh Ji Maharaj. It also carries historical connections to Maharaja Ranjit Singh Ji.
