OVERVIEW:
Punaura Dham, also known as Maa Janaki Janmabhoomi Temple, is a highly sacred Hindu pilgrimage site located in the Punaura village of Sitamarhi district, Bihar. It holds supreme religious significance as the believed birthplace of Mata Sita (Mother Sita) from the epic Ramayana.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Maa Janaki Janmabhoomi Temple at Punaura Dham is dedicated to Goddess Sita. It features a prominent temple structure surrounded by a well-maintained complex. This site attracts thousands of devotees who come to offer prayers at the very birthplace of Mata Sita. The serene atmosphere and religious importance make it one of the most critical spiritual destinations in the Mithila region.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It is an integral part of the Ramayana circuit and possesses deep religious and cultural significance for followers of Hinduism, honoring the birthplace of Goddess Sita.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to the legend, there was once a severe famine in the Mithila region. The local priest advised King Janak to plow the field to bring rain. When King Janak was ploughing the field at this location, an earthen pot came out of the ground. Inside the pot was Mother Sita in an infant state. She was thus discovered and adopted by King Janak.
