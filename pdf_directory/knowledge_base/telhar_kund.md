OVERVIEW:
Telhar Kund is a popular scenic waterfall found in the Kaimur district in Bihar. Situated on the Rohtas plateau, it cascades deeply into a natural pool surrounded by lush green forests and rocky cliffs. It is located in close proximity to the Durgavati River on Bhabua.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The attraction features a breathtaking waterfall cascading down the steep rocky cliffs of the Rohtas plateau into a deep, serene pool below. The surrounding landscape is densely forested, offering a pristine natural environment for tourists and nature enthusiasts. Its proximity to the Durgavati River enhances the scenic beauty of the location.
