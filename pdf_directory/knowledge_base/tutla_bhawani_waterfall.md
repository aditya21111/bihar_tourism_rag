OVERVIEW:
Tutla Bhawani Waterfall, also known as Tutrahi or Titula Dham, is a stunning natural wonder in Rohtas. Located amidst lush green areas, the waterfall creates a beautiful green valley between two mountain ranges.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located near Tilauthu, approximately 20 KM southwest of Dehri-on-Sone, Tutla Bhawani Waterfall is nestled amidst lush green areas. It offers a wonderful environment with the waterfall covered by two mountain ranges on the north-west and south-east, creating a magnificent green valley that stretches up to 1 mile.
