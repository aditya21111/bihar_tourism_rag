OVERVIEW:
The Deo Sun Temple in Aurangabad is one of the ancient Sun temples of the country. Built in the 8th century AD by Chandravanshi King Bhairavendra Singh. The 100 ft. high temple resembles the temple at Konark in its architectural form. A unique thing about the Deo Sun temple is that it faces west unlike the traditional Sun temples which face east.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Deo Sun Temple is an ancient and prominent Sun temple located in Aurangabad. It was built in the 8th century AD by the Chandravanshi King Bhairavendra Singh. The temple stands 100 ft. high and its architectural form closely resembles the famous temple at Konark. A highly unique feature of the Deo Sun temple is that it faces the west direction, unlike traditional Sun temples which typically face east.
