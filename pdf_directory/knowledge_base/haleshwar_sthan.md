OVERVIEW:
Haleshwar Sthan is an ancient and highly revered temple dedicated to Lord Shiva, located about 3 km north west of Sitamarhi. It holds deep religious and mythological significance as it is believed to have been founded by the King of Videha in ancient times on the occasion of the Putrayesthi Yagna. 

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located just 3 km north west of Sitamarhi, Haleshwar Sthan is a significant ancient temple dedicated to Lord Shiva. The temple complex is renowned for its traditional architectural structure and deep-rooted historical and spiritual ambience. Devotees visit this sacred site to offer their prayers to Lord Shiva, connecting with the rich mythological heritage of the Videha Kingdom.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The temple holds immense historical and mythological significance. It traces its origins back to the ancient Kingdom of Videha.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to prevailing myths and historical accounts, this ancient temple was founded by the King of Videha on the sacred occasion of the Putrayesthi Yagna. This legend establishes the site as one of prominent mythological importance.
