OVERVIEW:
Muchalind Tank is a serene and holy water body located in Bodhgaya. It is renowned for an incredible event that occurred during the sixth week after Lord Buddha attained enlightenment, involving his protection from heavy rains by a mythical snake king.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The tank commemorates a period of Buddha's post-enlightenment meditation. Following his enlightenment, Lord Buddha spent his 6th week in meditation at this location. It serves as a peaceful spot for visitors to reflect on the legendary stories of Buddha's deep meditation amidst the elements.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It highlights the deep harmony between the awakened Buddha and nature/mythological beings, serving as an important point in the seven-week post-enlightenment narrative at Bodhgaya.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to the legend, while Buddha was deep in meditation during his 6th week after enlightenment, a severe storm struck. It is said that a snake king named Muchalind emerged and protected the Buddha from the heavy rains, shielding him with his hood.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
* Site of Buddha's meditation during the 6th week after enlightenment.
* Legend of the snake king Muchalind protecting Buddha from the rain.
