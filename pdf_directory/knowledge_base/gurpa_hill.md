OVERVIEW:
Gurpa Hill, also known as Gurupada Giri or Kukkutpada Giri, is a highly revered natural and historical site in the Gaya district. It holds immense religious significance as the place where Mahakasyapa, the last disciple of Buddha, attained Nirvana. The hill is celebrated for its spiritual aura, breathtaking natural scenery, and a temple with footprints attributed to Lord Vishnu.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Gurpa Hill is a picturesque destination offering stunning natural scenery, lush forests, waterfalls, and beautiful sunrise and sunset views. The hill features a temple named Gurupada, which is highly visited by pilgrims. The area is steeped in Buddhist history, known originally as Gurupada Giri.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It is an essential pilgrimage site in Buddhism, known as the location where Buddha's prominent and last disciple, Mahakasyapa, attained Nirvana. The presence of the Gurupada temple further adds to its multi-religious significance.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to Buddhist traditions, Mahakasyapa attained Nirvana on this hill. Additionally, there is a belief that the Gurupada temple on the hill contains the footprints of Lord Vishnu, making it sacred for Hindus as well.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
* Site where Mahakasyapa attained Nirvana.
* Gurupada temple featuring the assumed footprints of "Lord Vishnu".
* Natural scenery including forests, waterfalls, and scenic sunrise/sunset views.
