OVERVIEW:
Abhishek Pushkarni, also known as the Coronation Tank, is an ancient sacred lake located in Vaishali. It holds profound historical importance from the ancient period, as its holy waters were used to bless the elected representatives of Vaishali before their oath-taking ceremonies in one of the world's first democratic republics.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The site is a historic water body known as a lake or tank. Its waters were considered holy in ancient times. It served as the central site for the coronation and oath-taking ceremonies of the Lichhavis' elected representatives. The newly built Vishwa Shanti Stupa is situated on one side of this sacred lake.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Vaishali is recognized as one of the first democratic republics in the world, thriving in the 6th century BCE under the Lichchavis and Vajjis. The Lichhavis came up with the first republic to rule the state and were avid followers of the Buddha. The tank represents the core of their democratic traditions and spiritual beliefs.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
It was believed that the water in the lake was holy, essential for blessing the elected representatives before they took their oath of office.

* Coronation site for the elected representatives of Vaishali's ancient republic.
* Situated adjacent to the Vishwa Shanti Stupa.
