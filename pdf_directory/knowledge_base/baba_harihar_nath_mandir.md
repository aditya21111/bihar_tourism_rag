OVERVIEW:
Baba Harihar Nath Mandir is a unique and significant religious site situated on the banks of the Gandak and Ganges rivers in Sonpur, Saran district. Dedicated to both Lord Vishnu (Hari) and Lord Shiva (Har), the temple features a unique deity where both gods are situated on the same stone, making it a powerful pilgrimage site for Hindu devotees.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Harihar Nath Temple is a prominent landmark in Sonpur, Bihar, beautifully located at the confluence or banks of the Gandak and Ganges rivers. The primary feature of the temple is its main deities: Lord (Hari) Vishnu and Lord (Har) Shiva. This distinctive union of two major Hindu gods in a single idol draws countless pilgrims and tourists who come to worship and seek blessings at this sacred riverside destination.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
It holds immense religious importance as a rare pilgrimage site that unifies Vaishnavism and Shaivism, symbolising the harmony between the followers of Lord Vishnu and Lord Shiva. 

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
It is believed that the Harihar Nath Temple is a unique pilgrimage site where both Hari (Vishnu) and Har (Shiva) are situated on the same stone, embodying their divine unity.
