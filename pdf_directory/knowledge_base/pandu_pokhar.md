OVERVIEW:
Pandu Pokhar is a historic and picturesque lake spread over 22 acres of land in Rajgir. Named after King Pandu, the lake combines beautiful scenic views of blooming lotus flowers with entertainment and camping facilities, making it a perfect destination for excursions and vacations.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Spread over an area of 22 acres, Pandu Pokhar is a historic lake that has been developed into an attractive tourist destination. The lake is especially famous for its beautiful lotus flowers that bloom during the season, adding to the scenic beauty of the area. It offers a unique combination of entertainment and camping options, creating a beautiful destination for family excursions and vacations.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
The lake is historic and is believed to have been named after King Pandu, the father of the Pandavas.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
The lake is said to be named after King Pandu, who is believed to have visited the pond during his exile.
