OVERVIEW:
Mithila Haat is located beside NH 57 at Araria Sangram in Jhanjharpur, Madhubani district. It has been constructed on the East West corridor to showcase the rich heritage of the Mithila area. The delectable cuisines of Mithila are served from 10 stalls constructed there. It is rapidly becoming a hot tourist spot where visitors can also enjoy boating in the "Badki Taalaab". A Craft Court showcases and sells internationally acclaimed Mithila painting and Mithila Craft. It has become a center of tourism in Bihar, attracting people from across the country and abroad. It introduces tourists to the village life and culture of Mithila, offering a chance to taste traditional dishes by sitting in the Bhansa Ghar (kitchen room).

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Mithila Haat offers a vibrant and immersive cultural experience dedicated to the heritage of the Mithila region. Situated conveniently beside NH 57 at Araria Sangram, it features 10 dedicated stalls serving traditional and delectable Mithila cuisines. Visitors can experience dining in the authentic setting of a Bhansa Ghar (kitchen room). The attraction includes the Craft Court, a dedicated space where the internationally renowned Mithila paintings and traditional Mithila crafts are both exhibited and sold. Beyond art and food, tourists can engage in recreational activities like boating in the prominent "Badki Taalaab", making it a comprehensive cultural and leisure destination.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Highly significant for preserving, showcasing, and promoting the rich cultural heritage, village lifestyle, traditional art (Mithila painting), and cuisine of the Mithila region.
