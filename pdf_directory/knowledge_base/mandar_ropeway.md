OVERVIEW:
The Mandar Ropeway in Banka enhances the accessibility of the sacred Mandar Hill. This aerial transport system provides a convenient and scenic journey for pilgrims and tourists, offering breathtaking views of the surrounding landscapes. The Mandar Ropeway has become an integral part of the pilgrimage experience to this revered site in Banka.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
An aerial transport cable car system that journeys up the sacred Mandar Hill. It is designed to facilitate easy access for pilgrims and tourists, allowing them to enjoy breathtaking panoramic views of the Banka region's landscapes while traveling to the hill.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Serves the sacred Mandar Hill, enhancing accessibility for pilgrims. Mandar Hill itself is a highly revered site, and the ropeway has become a crucial part of the traditional pilgrimage experience.
