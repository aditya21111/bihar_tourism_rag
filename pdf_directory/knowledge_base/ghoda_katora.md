OVERVIEW:
Ghoda Katora is a scenic lake and ecological restoration site in Rajgir, Nalanda. Meaning 'Horse's Bowl', the area is completely free of petrol/diesel vehicles and is a haven for migratory birds from Siberia and Central Asia, offering a pristine natural environment for visitors.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Ghoda Katora is declared an ecological restoration site by the Department of Tourism. To preserve its pristine environment, petrol and diesel vehicles are strictly prohibited; only horse carts, tongas, or e-rikshaws are allowed. The lake is surrounded by hills and features a beautiful statue of Buddha in the center. It is a prominent habitat for migratory birds from Siberia and Central Asia, making it a frequent visiting spot for bird lovers seeking glimpses of Siberian cranes and indigenous bird species.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Etymologically, Ghoda Katora means 'Horse's Bowl'. It is believed that in the ancient age, it was used as stables where the lake is now situated.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
Believed to have been the stables during the ancient age.
