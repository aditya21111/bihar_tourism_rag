Religious Site / Natural Attraction

OVERVIEW:
The Mahabodhi Tree in Bodhgaya is the most sacred natural monument in the Buddhist world. Located within the Mahabodhi Temple complex, it marks the exact location where Gautama Buddha sat and attained supreme knowledge and enlightenment. 

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
The Mahabodhi Tree is a large, ancient fig tree under which the Buddha meditated. Directly under the tree lies the Vajrasana, a stone platform known as the seat of stability. It was sitting on this very platform under the shading branches of the Bodhi Tree that the Buddha achieved enlightenment, making it the epicenter of the Buddhist faith.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
This tree represents the spiritual awakening of Gautama Buddha and the birth of Buddhism. It is universally venerated as a symbol of peace, purity, and supreme knowledge.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
According to Buddhist traditions, Gautama attained 'enlightenment' sitting under this specific tree and became the Buddha.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
* The sacred tree under which Buddha attained enlightenment.
* The Vajrasana (seat of stability) stone platform located under the tree.
