OVERVIEW:
Kaimur Wildlife Sanctuary is the largest sanctuary in Bihar, covering over 1505 square kilometers. It is a stunning natural preserve home to a wide variety of endangered species and wildlife, offering an unforgettable nature experience.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Covering a massive area of over 1505 square kilometers, the Kaimur Wildlife Sanctuary holds the distinction of being the largest sanctuary in the state. The sanctuary is a stunning natural preserve that provides habitat to numerous endangered species, including Chinkara, four-horned antelope, blue-bulls, sambar, cheetal, bears, and leopards. It is also home to a diverse population of reptiles like snakes, pythons, and crocodiles.
