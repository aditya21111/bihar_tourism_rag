OVERVIEW:
The Nagi Nakti Bird Sanctuary comprises two sanctuaries, Nagi Dam (791 ha) and Nakti Dam (332 ha), located very close to each other in Jamui district. Together they form a single significant bird area. These notified sanctuaries feature deep water bodies with clear surfaces, surrounded by rocky hillocks formed by the construction of dams on the streams.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Nagi and Nakti are two distinct but closely situated dam-based sanctuaries (Nagi is 7 Kms from Jhajha; Nakti is a further 4 Kms from Nagi). They share a similar habitat. The sanctuaries boast quite deep water bodies with clear water surfaces, enclosed by scenic rocky hillocks created due to the damming of local streams. The total area covers 791 ha for Nagi and 332 ha for Nakti.

RETRIEVAL SUMMARY:
Nagi Nakti Bird Sanctuary is a combined wildlife and natural attraction in Jamui district, Bihar, consisting of Nagi Dam (791 ha) and Nakti Dam (332 ha). The sanctuaries are known for their deep clear water bodies surrounded by rocky hillocks, providing a great habitat for birds. It is located near Jhajha (10 Kms from railway station) and best visited from October to March. Nearby attractions include Simultala Hill and Giddeshwar Temple.
