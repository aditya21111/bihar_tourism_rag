OVERVIEW:
Muzaffarpur is a major city in Bihar, serving as the headquarters of the Tirhut division. Globally renowned as the "Lychee Kingdom", it is the state's fourth most populous city and is famous for producing the premium Shahi lychee. Situated on the scenic banks of the Burhi Gandak River, it is a prominent agricultural and natural tourism destination.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
Located in the Tirhut region, Muzaffarpur is a vibrant city situated on the banks of the perennial Burhi Gandak River, which originates from the Someshwar Hills of the Himalayas. The city is the headquarters of the Tirhut division, Muzaffarpur district, and the Muzaffarpur Railway District. It is famously celebrated for its Shahi lychees, an exquisite fruit that positions the city as the Lychee Kingdom. 

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Muzaffarpur is an important administrative and historical center in the Tirhut region. Its Shahi litchi holds immense agricultural and cultural pride, set to become the fourth product from Bihar to receive the prestigious Geographical Indication (GI) tag (following jardalu mango, katarni rice, and Magahi paan).
