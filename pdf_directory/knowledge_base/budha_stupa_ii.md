OVERVIEW:
Budha Stupa-II is a significant archaeological site in Vaishali. Excavations at this stupa in the mid-20th century uncovered a casket believed to contain a part of the ashes of Lord Buddha, making it a critical pilgrimage point and historical monument on the Buddhist Circuit.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
This stupa stands as an important archaeological discovery. In the mid-20th century, excavation at the site of this stupa led to the discovery of another reliquary casket. This casket is widely believed to contain a portion of the sacred ashes of Lord Buddha.

HISTORICAL OR CULTURAL SIGNIFICANCE:
Like Budha Stupa-I, this stupa connects deeply with the history of Vaishali, an ancient city that hosted the second great Buddhist council. The unearthing of a casket with Buddha's ashes highlights its status as a venerated reliquary site built by early followers, likely the Lichhavis.

LEGENDS, STORIES, OR TRADITIONS:
Vaishali was frequently visited by Lord Buddha. To honor his connection with the people, stupas were constructed to enshrine his relics, preserving his presence in the region.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS:
* Mid-20th century excavation site.
* Discovery of a casket believed to contain a part of the ashes of Lord Buddha.
